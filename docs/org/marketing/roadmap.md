## Phases
1. **Warm-up: now → 6 Dec** (new, founder 2026-11-05). The app is live and friends are using it. Small and honest: real moments from community games, quick how-tos, the founder building in public. 5–7 posts a week. Audience: adult organisers and players of friends' and community games.
2. **Pre-launch: 7 Dec → 10 Jan** — the approved plan in [content-calendar.md](../../marketing/content-calendar.md), adjusted only by founder-approved changes.
3. **Launch week: 11–17 Jan.**
4. **First season: 18 Jan → 14 Mar.**
5. **Paid promotion:** after the founder decides, from what the numbers show works.

## Warm-up pillars
| Pillar | What | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Moment | a real or demo game moment, live | "Last point, 20–20. Everyone watching from the link." |
| How-to | one task in 15–30 s | "Score a badminton game from your phone" |
| The link | following a game from a link, no app needed | "Send one link to the group chat" |
| Builds itself | results, standings, stats with no extra work | "The table updated before we left the court" |
| Building in public | the founder, what's new this week | LinkedIn founder posts |
