# Recording checklists: posts 1 and 5

Both need two phones (or a phone + a laptop browser) and the **marketing demo account** with fictional adult players (backlog M-07). Before recording, on both phones: Do Not Disturb on, battery and time look normal, no notifications, no real contacts or chats on screen. Use a demo group chat with no real people in it (e.g. a WhatsApp group with only yourself named "Sunday 5-a-side").

## Post 1: "The score link in the group chat" — 15 s, Instagram + Facebook Reel (Tue 10 Nov, 19:00)
Screen-record on **phone A** (scorer) and **phone B** (viewer); edit together.
1. Phone A: on the demo account, Home → **Score a match** → Football, two demo sides (e.g. "Red" v "Blue"), start. Score to **2–1**. *(Do this before recording.)*
2. **Record A (5 s):** the live score 2–1 → tap **Share** → choose the demo group chat → send.
3. **Record B (5 s):** the chat → tap the link → it opens in the browser showing **2–1**. Keep the match number in the address bar out of shot, or blur it in editing.
4. **Record A + B (5 s):** score a goal on A → B changes to **3–1** by itself.
5. Edit (Instagram's Reel editor or Canva): text overlays "Your mates can't make it?" → "Tap Share" → "They watch live. No app needed." End card: SportnNote logo + "app.sportnnote.in". Small "Demo data" label in a corner throughout. No voice needed; add a light music track from Instagram's library.

## Post 5: "Share a live score with anyone. No app needed." — 20 s, YouTube Short (Fri 13 Nov, 18:00)
1. Phone A: Home → **Score a match** → Basketball, demo sides, start. Score to **44–41** before recording.
2. **Record A (0:00–0:08):** the live score 44–41 → tap **Share** → send to the demo chat.
3. **Record B (0:08–0:15):** open the link in the browser (blur the match number) → it shows 44–41 → on A, score a three → B shows **47–41**.
4. End card (0:15–0:20): SportnNote logo, "app.sportnnote.in", "Subscribe for more how-tos".
5. Voice (your own, recorded on the phone) — optional; or text on screen only:
   - "Want the whole group to follow the game?"
   - "Tap Share and send the link."
   - "They open it in a browser. No app. It updates as you score."
   - "Subscribe for more how-tos."
6. Upload as a Short; "Altered or synthetic content": **No**.
