## 1. Definitions

1.1 "**Intellectual Property**" means copyright (including in computer programmes, which are literary works under the Copyright Act, 1957), patents and inventions, designs, trade marks, service marks, trade names, logos and get-up, together with their goodwill, domain names, database rights, trade secrets and know-how, and all other similar rights anywhere in the world. It covers registered and unregistered rights, applications and the right to apply, and the right to sue for past, present and future infringement.

1.2 "**Assigned IP**" means all Intellectual Property in SportnNote and in everything listed in Schedule 1. It includes all versions, drafts, improvements and works in progress existing on the date of this Deed, and anything the Founder later creates for SportnNote before becoming the Company's employee.

1.3 "**Effective Date**" means [the date of this Deed / the date of incorporation of the Company].

## 2. Assignment

2.1 **What is assigned:**
- (a) In return for the consideration in clause 3, the Founder hereby assigns, transfers and conveys to the Company, absolutely and with full title guarantee, all of the Founder's right, title and interest in the Assigned IP, with effect from the Effective Date.
- (b) For copyright, this includes every right under section 14 of the Copyright Act, 1957, in every form, medium and mode, whether known now or developed later.
- (c) For trade marks, this includes the goodwill of the business connected with them.
- (d) For future works under clause 1.2, the assignment takes effect as each work comes into existence (section 18).

2.2 **No lapse or limits:**
- (a) For each right, the assignment is worldwide and lasts for the full term of that right, including all renewals and extensions.
- (b) It does not lapse under section 19(4) of the Copyright Act, 1957 if the Company does not exercise any right within one year.
- (c) It is not limited to 5 years under section 19(5) or to India under section 19(6).

2.3 **Rights against infringers:** The Company may sue and recover damages for any infringement of the Assigned IP, including infringement before the Effective Date.

2.4 **AI-assisted work:**
- (a) Parts of SportnNote were written or drafted with the help of AI tools, including Anthropic's Claude, used under terms that give the user the rights in the outputs.
- (b) The Founder assigns all rights they hold in that material, as part of the Assigned IP.
- (c) To the extent any such material is not protected by copyright, the Founder transfers to the Company all their interest in it, including as confidential information and trade secrets, and shall keep it confidential.

2.5 **Business and personal data:**
- (a) The Founder transfers to the Company the operation of the SportnNote service, including its databases, so that the Company is the Data Fiduciary for the personal data in them from the Effective Date under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
- (b) The Company shall continue to process that personal data only for the purposes stated in SportnNote's privacy notice.
- (c) The Parties shall update SportnNote's privacy notice and terms to name the Company, and shall inform users as the law requires.

## 5. The Founder's confirmations

5.1 The Founder confirms that, to the best of the Founder's knowledge:
- (a) the Founder is the sole owner of the Assigned IP and has not assigned, licensed or charged it to anyone else, apart from the contributors and third-party components listed in Schedule 2;
- (b) the Assigned IP was created by the Founder, or by the contributors listed in Schedule 2. Each contributor has assigned, or will before [DATE] assign, their rights to the Founder or the Company;
- (c) the Assigned IP was not created in the course of any employment, or using any employer's or third party's time, equipment, facilities, paid tools or confidential information;
- (d) no one has made any claim against the Assigned IP, and the Founder knows of no infringement of it or by it; and
- (e) the third-party and open-source components in SportnNote are used under their own licences, which are not assigned by this Deed. The Founder has not knowingly breached any of those licences.

5.2 Third-party components listed in Schedule 2 remain owned by their owners, and the Company takes the benefit of their licences as far as they allow.

## 6. Moral rights

The Founder agrees, to the extent the law allows, not to assert any moral rights (including under section 57 of the Copyright Act, 1957) in the Assigned IP. The Founder consents to the Company modifying, adapting or combining it, and to publishing it without naming the Founder.

## 7. Confidentiality

From the Effective Date, all non-public information in the Assigned IP is the Company's Confidential Information. The Founder shall keep it confidential and use it only for the Company's benefit. This does not limit the Founder's role in the Company.

## 8. Further assurance and power of attorney

8.1 **Further assurance:** At the Company's request and cost, the Founder shall sign every document and do everything reasonably needed to vest, record, register and enforce the Company's rights in the Assigned IP in any country. This includes forms to record the assignment of trade marks (Trade Marks Act, 1999, section 45), patents (Patents Act, 1970, section 68), designs and copyright.

8.2 **Power of attorney:** The Founder irrevocably appoints the Company, acting through any director other than the Founder, as the Founder's attorney to sign and do those things in the Founder's name if the Founder does not do so within 7 days of a written request. This appointment is given as security for the Company's interest in the Assigned IP.

## 9. General

9.1 **Law and courts:** This Deed is governed by the laws of India. The courts at Hyderabad, Telangana have exclusive jurisdiction.

9.2 **Whole agreement:** This Deed is the whole agreement between the Parties about the Assigned IP.

9.3 **No waiver; severability:** A delay in enforcing a right is not a waiver of it. If any part is held unenforceable, the rest continues, and the unenforceable part is read down to the extent needed.

9.4 **Stamp duty and copies:** The Company shall pay the stamp duty and any registration costs. This Deed is executed in two originals, one for each Party.
