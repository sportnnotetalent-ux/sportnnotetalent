The Parties wish to share Confidential Information with each other only for the following purpose: [PURPOSE, e.g. "discussing an integration between SportnNote and the Partner's league-management system" / "a possible sponsorship of school tournaments"] (the "**Purpose**").

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "**Confidential Information**" means all information that is not public and that a Discloser (or anyone on its behalf) discloses to the Recipient, or that the Recipient sees or obtains through access to the Discloser's systems, before or after the date of this Agreement, in any form and whether or not marked confidential. It includes:

- (a) source code, object code, database schemas, APIs, keys and credentials, system architecture, algorithms and models;
- (b) product plans, roadmaps, unreleased features, designs and research;
- (c) business, marketing, pricing, financial and legal information;
- (d) information about the Discloser's users, players, members and customers, including personal data and, in particular, children's data;
- (e) the existence and content of the Parties' discussions; and
- (f) anything derived from the above, including notes, analyses, summaries, screenshots and outputs of software or AI tools.

1.2 Confidential Information does not include information that the Recipient shows, with written records, (a) is or becomes public other than through a breach by the Recipient or its Representatives; (b) was lawfully known to the Recipient before disclosure, without any duty of confidence; (c) was lawfully received from a third party without any duty of confidence; or (d) was independently developed by the Recipient without using or referring to the Discloser's Confidential Information. These exceptions never apply to personal data.

1.3 "**Representatives**" means a Party's employees, officers, contractors and professional advisers who need to know the Confidential Information for the Purpose and are bound by written confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Agreement.

## 2. Each Recipient's obligations

Each Recipient shall:

2.1 use the Discloser's Confidential Information only for the Purpose;

2.2 keep it secret and protect it with at least the care it uses for its own most sensitive information, and never less than reasonable care;

2.3 disclose it only to its Representatives. It is responsible for any breach of this Agreement by its Representatives as if it were its own breach;

2.4 not copy or record it except as strictly needed for the Purpose, and not remove any confidentiality or ownership notice;

2.5 not reverse engineer, decompile or disassemble the Discloser's software, and not scrape, crawl, bulk-download or extract data from the Discloser's apps, websites, APIs or databases, nor try to get around any access control, except as the Discloser expressly authorises in writing;

2.6 not use the Discloser's Confidential Information to build, help build or improve any product or service that competes with the Discloser's, or to apply for any patent, design or trade mark;

2.7 **AI tools:** not enter, upload or paste any of the Discloser's Confidential Information into any AI model, chatbot, coding assistant, translation tool or other third-party online service, unless (a) the Discloser has approved that specific tool in writing, and (b) the tool is set up so that inputs and outputs are not used to train or improve any model and are not kept longer than needed to provide the service;

2.8 **Personal data:** if it receives or can see any personal data from the Discloser:
- (a) process it only for the Purpose and only as the Discloser instructs, with reasonable security safeguards, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the rules under it (and any other data protection law that applies to the Discloser);
- (b) never try to identify, contact, profile, track or target any child; and
- (c) tell the Discloser within 24 hours of becoming aware of any actual or suspected personal data breach, and delete the personal data when the Discloser asks; and

2.9 tell the Discloser promptly if it learns of any unauthorised use or disclosure, and help the Discloser to limit it.

## 3. Disclosure required by law

If a law, court or regulator requires a Recipient to disclose Confidential Information, it shall (where lawful) give the Discloser prompt written notice, disclose only the minimum required, and cooperate with any effort by the Discloser to obtain a protective order.

## 4. Return or destruction

When the Discloser asks, or when the Purpose ends, the Recipient shall within 7 days return or securely destroy all of the Discloser's Confidential Information and every copy, note and derivative in its control (including chat or AI-tool histories it can delete), and confirm this in writing. Copies that cannot practically be deleted from automatic back-ups may be kept only until they are overwritten, and stay subject to this Agreement.

## 5. Ownership and no licence

Each Party's Confidential Information remains its property. This Agreement gives no licence or right in it except to use it for the Purpose. Neither Party has a duty to disclose anything or to enter into any further agreement. Confidential Information is provided "as is".

## 6. Feedback

Either Party may freely use any idea, suggestion or feedback about its own products that the other Party gives, without payment or attribution, and the Party giving it agrees not to assert any rights in it, including moral rights, to the extent the law allows.

## 7. Independent development

Nothing in this Agreement stops either Party from developing products similar to those discussed, so long as it does not use or refer to the other Party's Confidential Information. There is no "residuals" right: information kept in a person's memory is still Confidential Information.

## 8. Duration

8.1 This Agreement covers Confidential Information disclosed during [2 years] from its date. Either Party may end it earlier by 30 days' written notice.

8.2 The obligations continue for 5 years after the last disclosure. For source code, credentials, personal data and trade secrets, they continue for as long as that information remains confidential.

## 9. Remedies

Each Party acknowledges that a breach of this Agreement may cause harm that money cannot adequately make good. In addition to any other remedy, the injured Party may seek an injunction and specific performance under the Specific Relief Act, 1963. A Party is liable for losses the other suffers from a breach by it or its Representatives.

## 10. General

10.1 **Law and courts:** This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. The courts at Hyderabad, Telangana have exclusive jurisdiction, except that either Party may also seek urgent interim relief in any competent court.

10.2 **Whole agreement:** This Agreement is the whole agreement between the Parties about its subject matter. It can be changed only in writing signed by both Parties.

10.3 **No waiver; severability:** A delay in enforcing a right is not a waiver of it. If any part of this Agreement is held unenforceable, the rest continues, and the unenforceable part is read down to the extent needed.

10.4 **Assignment:** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other's written consent, except that the Company may assign it to any successor to the SportnNote business or to its affiliates.

10.5 **Notices:** Notices must be in writing, by email to the addresses below with a copy by post or courier if the other Party asks.

10.6 **Signatures and stamp duty:** This Agreement may be signed in counterparts and by electronic signature under the Information Technology Act, 2000. The Parties will share the stamp duty equally [or: the Company will pay it].
