The Company is willing to share Confidential Information with the Recipient only for the following purpose: [PURPOSE, e.g. "evaluating a freelance engagement to build features for SportnNote" / "testing unreleased features of the SportnNote app" / "advising the Company on its go-to-market plan"] (the "**Purpose**").

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "**Confidential Information**" means all information that is not public and that the Company (or anyone on its behalf) discloses to the Recipient, or that the Recipient sees or obtains through access to the Company's systems, before or after the date of this Agreement, in any form and whether or not marked confidential. It includes:

- (a) source code, object code, database schemas, migrations, APIs, keys and credentials, system architecture, algorithms, scoring and rules engines, statistics models and test data;
- (b) product plans, roadmaps, unreleased features, designs, prototypes and research;
- (c) business, marketing, pricing, financial, fundraising and legal information;
- (d) information about users, players, parents, schools, clubs and other customers, including personal data and, in particular, children's data;
- (e) the existence and content of discussions between the Parties; and
- (f) anything derived from the above, including notes, analyses, summaries, screenshots and outputs of software or AI tools.

1.2 Confidential Information does not include information that the Recipient shows, with written records, (a) is or becomes public other than through a breach by the Recipient or its Representatives; (b) was lawfully known to the Recipient before the Company disclosed it, without any duty of confidence; (c) was lawfully received from a third party without any duty of confidence; or (d) was independently developed by the Recipient without using or referring to the Confidential Information. These exceptions never apply to personal data.

1.3 "**Representatives**" means, where the Recipient is an entity, its employees, officers and professional advisers who need to know the Confidential Information for the Purpose and are bound by written confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Agreement.

## 2. The Recipient's obligations

The Recipient shall:

2.1 use the Confidential Information only for the Purpose;

2.2 keep the Confidential Information secret and protect it with at least the care it uses for its own most sensitive information, and never less than reasonable care;

2.3 not disclose it to anyone except, where the Recipient is an entity, its Representatives. The Recipient is responsible for any breach of this Agreement by its Representatives as if it were its own breach;

2.4 not copy or record the Confidential Information except as strictly needed for the Purpose, and not remove any confidentiality or ownership notice;

2.5 not reverse engineer, decompile or disassemble any SportnNote software, and not scrape, crawl, bulk-download or extract data from the Company's apps, websites, APIs or databases, nor try to get around any access control, except as the Company expressly authorises in writing;

2.6 not use the Confidential Information to build, help build or improve any product or service that competes with SportnNote, or to apply for any patent, design or trade mark;

2.7 **AI tools:** not enter, upload or paste any Confidential Information (including code, schemas, documents and screenshots) into any AI model, chatbot, coding assistant, translation tool or other third-party online service, unless (a) the Company has approved that specific tool in writing, and (b) the tool is set up so that inputs and outputs are not used to train or improve any model and are not kept longer than needed to provide the service;

2.8 **Personal data:** if the Recipient receives or can see any personal data:
- (a) process it only for the Purpose and only as the Company instructs, with reasonable security safeguards, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the rules under it;
- (b) never try to identify, contact, profile, track or target any child; and
- (c) tell the Company within 24 hours of becoming aware of any actual or suspected personal data breach, and delete the personal data when the Company asks; and

2.9 tell the Company promptly if it learns of any unauthorised use or disclosure of Confidential Information, and help the Company to limit it.

## 3. Disclosure required by law

If a law, court or regulator requires the Recipient to disclose Confidential Information, the Recipient shall (where lawful) give the Company prompt written notice, disclose only the minimum required, and cooperate with any effort by the Company to obtain a protective order.

## 4. Return or destruction

When the Company asks, or when the Purpose ends, the Recipient shall within 7 days return or securely destroy all Confidential Information and every copy, note and derivative in its control (including chat or AI-tool histories it can delete), and confirm this in writing. Copies that cannot practically be deleted from automatic back-ups may be kept only until they are overwritten, and stay subject to this Agreement.

## 5. Ownership and no licence

All Confidential Information remains the Company's property. This Agreement gives the Recipient no licence or right in it except to use it for the Purpose. The Company has no duty to disclose anything or to enter into any further agreement. Confidential Information is provided "as is".

## 6. Feedback

The Company may freely use any idea, suggestion or feedback about SportnNote that the Recipient gives ("**Feedback**"), without payment or attribution. The Recipient hereby assigns to the Company all rights in the Feedback, worldwide and for the full term of those rights. The assignment does not lapse under section 19(4) of the Copyright Act, 1957 if the Company does not use the Feedback within one year. The Recipient agrees not to assert any moral rights in the Feedback, to the extent the law allows.

## 7. Non-solicitation [OPTIONAL]

For 12 months after the Purpose ends, the Recipient shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit for employment or engagement any employee or contractor of the Company with whom the Recipient dealt for the Purpose. This does not stop general advertisements that are not targeted at them.
