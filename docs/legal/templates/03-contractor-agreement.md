## Background

The Company owns and operates **SportnNote**, a multi-sport scoring, tournament-management and athlete-records platform (its mobile apps, the websites at sportnnote.in and app.sportnnote.in, and the systems behind them). The Company wishes to engage the Contractor to provide the services described in Schedule 1, and the Contractor agrees that everything it creates for the Company belongs to the Company.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "**Services**" means the services described in Schedule 1, and any further services the Parties agree in writing.

1.2 "**Deliverables**" means everything the Contractor (or anyone working for it) creates, alone or with others, in performing the Services or that relates to SportnNote. It includes source code, scripts, configuration, database migrations, tests, designs, graphics, user-interface text, documentation, data, inventions and improvements, and all drafts and working files.

1.3 "**Intellectual Property**" means copyright (including in computer programmes, which are literary works under the Copyright Act, 1957), patents and inventions, designs, trade marks, database rights, trade secrets and know-how, and all other similar rights anywhere in the world, whether registered or not, including applications and the right to apply.

1.4 "**Background IP**" means Intellectual Property that the Contractor owned before this Agreement or creates outside it without using the Company's Confidential Information, as listed in Schedule 2.

1.5 "**Confidential Information**" means all information that is not public and that the Company discloses to the Contractor or that the Contractor sees or obtains through the Services or access to the Company's systems. It includes code, schemas, credentials, architecture, plans, roadmaps, designs, business information, the Deliverables, and information about users, players, parents, schools and clubs, including personal data and children's data. It excludes information that the Contractor shows, with written records, is or becomes public other than through its breach, or was lawfully known to it before the Company disclosed it, without any duty of confidence. Those exclusions never apply to personal data.

## 2. Services

2.1 The Contractor shall provide the Services and Deliverables described in Schedule 1, with reasonable skill, care and diligence, by the agreed dates.

2.2 Any change to the Services, fees or dates must be agreed in writing (email is enough).

2.3 The Contractor shall follow the Company's reasonable instructions on how work is delivered, including using the Company's code repositories, issue tracker and review process.

## 3. Fees and payment

3.1 The Company shall pay the fees in Schedule 1 within [15] days of receiving a correct invoice for work that is not in dispute.

3.2 The fees are the full payment for the Services and for the assignment and waivers in clause 5. No other payment, royalty or fee is due for the Deliverables or their use.

3.3 GST is added only if the Contractor is registered for it and shows it on a valid tax invoice. The Company may deduct tax at source where the law requires and will provide the certificate.

3.4 The Company reimburses expenses only if it approves them in writing beforehand.

## 4. Independent contractor

4.1 The Contractor is an independent contractor, not an employee, partner or agent of the Company. It is responsible for its own taxes, statutory contributions and insurance, and has no authority to bind the Company.

4.2 The Contractor confirms that taking on the Services does not breach any obligation it owes to anyone else (including any employer). It will not use any third party's time, equipment, confidential information or code in the Services. If the Contractor is employed elsewhere, it confirms that its employer's rules allow this work, and will show the Company written permission if asked.

## 5. Ownership of the Deliverables and assignment of Intellectual Property

5.1 **The Company owns the Deliverables.** All Intellectual Property in the Deliverables belongs to the Company from the moment each Deliverable is created.

5.2 **Assignment:**
- (a) The Contractor hereby assigns to the Company, absolutely, all its right, title and interest in all Intellectual Property in the Deliverables, both existing ones and future ones as they come into existence (Copyright Act, 1957, section 18).
- (b) For copyright, this includes every right under section 14 of that Act in every form, medium and mode, whether known now or developed later, including the rights to reproduce, adapt, translate, sell, license and communicate the Deliverables to the public.
- (c) For each right, the assignment is worldwide and lasts for the full term of that right, including any renewals and extensions.

5.3 **No lapse or limits:**
- (a) The assignment does not lapse under section 19(4) of the Copyright Act, 1957, even if the Company does not exercise any right within one year.
- (b) It is not limited to 5 years under section 19(5).
- (c) It is not limited to India under section 19(6).

5.4 **Moral rights:** The Contractor agrees, to the extent the law allows, not to assert any moral rights (including under section 57 of the Copyright Act, 1957) in the Deliverables. The Contractor consents to the Company modifying, adapting or combining them, and to publishing them without naming the Contractor.

5.5 **Further assurance and power of attorney:**
- (a) **Further assurance:** At the Company's request and cost, the Contractor shall sign any document and do anything reasonably needed to confirm, register or enforce the Company's rights, during and after this Agreement. This includes patent, design, copyright and trade mark filings and recordals in any country.
- (b) **Power of attorney:** If the Contractor does not do so within 7 days of a written request, the Contractor irrevocably appoints the Company, acting through any director, as its attorney to sign and do those things in its name. The Contractor confirms this appointment is given as security for the Company's interest in the Deliverables.

5.6 **Inventions:** The Contractor shall promptly tell the Company in writing about any invention or improvement made in the Services, and shall not file any patent or other application for it.

5.7 **Hand-over:**
- (a) The Contractor shall keep all source code and working files in the Company's repositories and systems (not personal ones) as work progresses.
- (b) The Contractor shall hand over everything else, including documentation and any credentials it created, whenever asked and at the end of this Agreement.
- (c) The Contractor shall not keep copies afterwards.

5.8 **Accounts:** Any account, domain, app-store listing, cloud project or third-party service set up for the Services must be set up in the Company's name or organisation. Anything set up in the Contractor's name is held on trust for the Company, and the Contractor shall transfer it to the Company when asked.

## 6. Background IP, open source and AI tools

6.1 **Background IP:** The Contractor keeps its Background IP. If any Background IP is included in a Deliverable, the Contractor grants the Company a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, transferable licence, with the right to sublicense, to use, copy, modify and distribute it as part of SportnNote. The Contractor shall not include any Background IP that is not listed in Schedule 2 without the Company's written approval.

6.2 **Open source:**
- (a) Without the Company's prior written approval, the Contractor shall include third-party or open-source material only under permissive licences (such as MIT, BSD, ISC or Apache-2.0).
- (b) It shall never include material under the GPL, AGPL, LGPL, SSPL, any other "copyleft" licence, or any "non-commercial" licence without that approval.
- (c) It shall keep a list of every third-party component it adds, with its licence.

6.3 **AI tools:**
- (a) **Approved tools only:** The Contractor may use AI coding or writing assistants only if the Company has approved the specific tool in writing, the tool is set up so that the Company's code, data and documents are not used to train or improve any model, and the tool's terms give the user the rights to its outputs for commercial use.
- (b) **Same standards:** The Contractor is responsible for AI-assisted output exactly as for its own work, and the warranties in clause 9 apply to it.
- (c) **Disclosure:** The Contractor shall tell the Company, if asked, which parts of a Deliverable were substantially generated by AI.
- (d) **No unapproved tools:** The Contractor shall not enter, upload or paste Confidential Information into any AI tool or online service that the Company has not approved.

## 7. Confidentiality

7.1 The Contractor shall:
- (a) use Confidential Information only to provide the Services, and keep it secret;
- (b) not disclose it to anyone except its own staff who need it for the Services and are bound by written obligations at least as protective as this clause. The Contractor is responsible for them;
- (c) not reverse engineer, decompile or disassemble SportnNote, or scrape or bulk-extract data from its apps, websites, APIs or databases, except as the Services require and the Company authorises; and
- (d) not use Confidential Information to build, help build or improve any competing product or service.

7.2 If a law, court or regulator requires disclosure, the Contractor shall (where lawful) give the Company prompt notice and disclose only the minimum required.

7.3 These obligations continue after this Agreement ends: for 5 years for general business information, and for as long as the information remains confidential for source code, credentials, personal data and trade secrets.

## 8. Data protection and security

8.1 **Data processor:** For any personal data the Contractor can access, the Company is the Data Fiduciary and the Contractor acts as its Data Processor under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the rules under it. The Contractor shall process personal data only on the Company's documented instructions and only to provide the Services.

8.2 **Children:** SportnNote holds children's data. The Contractor shall access only the minimum personal data it needs, and shall never contact, profile, track or target any child or use children's data for any other purpose.

8.3 **Security:** The Contractor shall:
- (a) use multi-factor authentication on every account that gives access to the Company's systems, and never share passwords;
- (b) never put keys, passwords or other secrets in code or documents;
- (c) not copy production data onto personal devices or into systems the Company has not approved, and use test or demo data for development wherever possible; and
- (d) keep its devices encrypted, locked and up to date.

8.4 **Breaches:** The Contractor shall tell the Company within 24 hours of becoming aware of any actual or suspected personal data breach or security incident, keep evidence, and help the Company respond.

8.5 **Subcontractors:** The Contractor shall not use any subcontractor or sub-processor without the Company's written approval, and remains responsible for any it uses.

8.6 **At the end:** When this Agreement ends, the Contractor shall delete all personal data and credentials it holds, and confirm this in writing. The Company will revoke the Contractor's access.

## 9. Warranties

The Contractor warrants that:
- (a) the Deliverables are its original work, apart from material allowed under clause 6;
- (b) the Deliverables and their use by the Company will not infringe anyone's Intellectual Property or other rights;
- (c) it has the right to enter into this Agreement and to make the assignment in clause 5;
- (d) the Deliverables contain no malware, back door, time bomb, hidden data collection or other harmful code; and
- (e) it will comply with all applicable laws in providing the Services.

## 10. Indemnity and liability

10.1 The Contractor shall indemnify the Company against all losses, costs and claims arising from:
- (a) any claim that a Deliverable infringes a third party's rights. This does not apply to material the Company supplied or to changes the Company directed; and
- (b) any breach of clauses 7 or 8.

10.2 The Company's total liability to the Contractor under this Agreement is limited to the fees payable under it.

## 11. Restrictions [OPTIONAL]

11.1 During this Agreement, the Contractor shall not, without the Company's written consent, provide services for any multi-sport scoring, tournament-management or athlete-records product that competes with SportnNote.

11.2 For 12 months after this Agreement ends, the Contractor shall not solicit for employment or engagement any employee or contractor of the Company with whom it worked. This does not stop general advertisements that are not targeted at them.

11.3 Nothing in this Agreement restricts the Contractor from working in its trade after this Agreement ends. Only the confidentiality and Intellectual Property obligations, and clause 11.2, continue after it ends.

## 12. Term and termination

12.1 This Agreement starts on the date above and continues until the Services are complete, unless ended earlier under this clause.

12.2 Either Party may end it by [14] days' written notice. The Company may end it immediately by written notice if the Contractor materially breaches it.

12.3 When it ends:
- (a) the Company shall pay for work properly done up to the end date;
- (b) all Intellectual Property in that work belongs to the Company under clause 5; and
- (c) the Contractor shall complete the hand-over in clauses 5.7 and 8.6 within 3 days.

12.4 Clauses 1 and 5 to 13 survive the end of this Agreement.

## 13. General

13.1 **Law and courts:** This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. The courts at Hyderabad, Telangana have exclusive jurisdiction, except that the Company may also seek urgent interim relief, including an injunction under the Specific Relief Act, 1963, in any competent court.

13.2 **Whole agreement:** This Agreement, with its Schedules, is the whole agreement between the Parties about its subject matter. It replaces any earlier non-disclosure agreement between them about the Services, without affecting obligations that arose under it. It can be changed only in writing signed by both Parties.

13.3 **No waiver; severability:** A delay in enforcing a right is not a waiver of it. If any part is held unenforceable, the rest continues, and the unenforceable part is read down to the extent needed.

13.4 **Assignment:** The Contractor may not assign or subcontract this Agreement without the Company's written consent. The Company may assign it to any successor to the SportnNote business or to its affiliates.

13.5 **Notices:** Notices must be in writing, by email to the addresses below.

13.6 **Stamp duty and copies:** The Company will pay the stamp duty. This Agreement is signed in two originals, one for each Party.
