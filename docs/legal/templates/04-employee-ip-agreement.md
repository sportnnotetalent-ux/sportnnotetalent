## Background

The Company owns and operates **SportnNote**, a multi-sport scoring, tournament-management and athlete-records platform. The Company has engaged the Employee as [ROLE] under an [appointment / internship] letter dated [DATE] (the "**Appointment Letter**"). This Agreement forms part of the Employee's terms of engagement, and the Employee's engagement and pay are the consideration for it.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "**Confidential Information**" means all information that is not public and that the Employee receives, sees or creates in connection with the engagement, in any form and whether or not marked confidential. It includes:
- (a) source code, schemas, credentials and architecture;
- (b) product plans, roadmaps, designs and research;
- (c) business, financial, fundraising and legal information;
- (d) information about colleagues, users, players, parents, schools and clubs, including personal data and children's data; and
- (e) the Company Work Product.

It excludes information that is or becomes public other than through a breach by the Employee. That exclusion never applies to personal data.

1.2 "**Company Work Product**" means every work, invention, improvement, design, piece of code, document and dataset that the Employee makes, alone or with others, during the engagement, which:
- (a) results from work done for the Company;
- (b) relates to the Company's actual or planned business, including SportnNote; or
- (c) is made using the Company's equipment, systems, accounts, paid tools, time or Confidential Information.

1.3 "**Intellectual Property**" means copyright (including in computer programmes, which are literary works under the Copyright Act, 1957), patents and inventions, designs, trade marks, database rights, trade secrets and know-how, and all other similar rights anywhere in the world, whether registered or not, including applications and the right to apply.

1.4 "**Excluded Work**" means work that the Employee makes entirely on their own time, without using any of the Company's equipment, systems, accounts, paid tools or Confidential Information, and that does not relate to the Company's actual or planned business. It also includes the prior works listed in Schedule 1.

## 2. Ownership of Company Work Product

2.1 **The Company owns it:** Company Work Product made in the course of the Employee's engagement belongs to the Company as its first owner (Copyright Act, 1957, section 17(c)).

2.2 **Assignment:**
- (a) To the extent any Intellectual Property in Company Work Product does not belong to the Company automatically, the Employee hereby assigns it to the Company absolutely. This covers existing works, and future works as they come into existence (section 18).
- (b) For each right, the assignment is worldwide, lasts for the full term of that right, and covers every form, medium and mode, whether known now or developed later.

2.3 **No lapse or limits:**
- (a) The assignment does not lapse under section 19(4) of the Copyright Act, 1957 if the Company does not exercise a right within one year.
- (b) It is not limited to 5 years under section 19(5) or to India under section 19(6).

2.4 **No extra payment:** No payment beyond the Employee's pay is due for Company Work Product or its use.

2.5 **Excluded Work:** Excluded Work stays the Employee's own. If the Employee puts any Excluded Work, or anything else they own, into Company Work Product, they grant the Company a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, transferable licence, with the right to sublicense, to use, copy, modify and distribute it.

## 3. Moral rights

The Employee agrees, to the extent the law allows, not to assert any moral rights (including under section 57 of the Copyright Act, 1957) in Company Work Product. The Employee consents to the Company modifying, adapting or combining it, and to publishing it without naming the Employee.

## 4. Disclosure, assistance and power of attorney

4.1 **Disclosure and assistance:**
- (a) The Employee shall promptly tell the Company in writing about any invention or improvement in the Company Work Product.
- (b) The Employee shall not file any patent, design or trade mark application for it.
- (c) At the Company's request and cost, during and after the engagement, the Employee shall sign any document and do anything reasonably needed to confirm, register or enforce the Company's rights.

4.2 **Power of attorney:** If the Employee does not do so within 7 days of a written request, the Employee irrevocably appoints the Company, acting through any director, as their attorney to sign and do those things in their name. This appointment is given as security for the Company's interest in the Company Work Product.

## 5. Confidentiality

5.1 During and after the engagement, the Employee shall:
- (a) use Confidential Information only for the Company's work, and keep it secret;
- (b) not disclose it to anyone outside the Company except as their role requires and the Company authorises; and
- (c) not copy it to personal devices, personal accounts or personal email.

5.2 If a law, court or regulator requires disclosure, the Employee shall (where lawful) tell the Company promptly and disclose only the minimum required.

5.3 These obligations continue after the engagement ends: for as long as the information remains confidential for source code, credentials, personal data and trade secrets, and for 5 years for other information.

## 6. AI tools, open source, security and data protection

6.1 **AI tools:** The Employee shall not enter, upload or paste Confidential Information into any AI model, chatbot, coding assistant or other online service that the Company has not approved in writing. Approved tools must be used only with the Company's accounts and settings, so that inputs are not used to train or improve any model.

6.2 **Open source:** The Employee shall not add copyleft-licensed (GPL, AGPL, LGPL, SSPL and similar) or "non-commercial" licensed material to Company Work Product without written approval, and shall record the licence of every third-party component they add.

6.3 **Security:** The Employee shall:
- (a) use multi-factor authentication on every Company account, and never share passwords;
- (b) never put secrets in code or documents;
- (c) keep production data only in approved systems, and use test or demo data for development wherever possible; and
- (d) keep work devices encrypted, locked and up to date.

6.4 **Personal data and children:** The Employee shall:
- (a) process personal data only as their role requires and the Company instructs, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the rules under it;
- (b) access only the minimum data needed;
- (c) never contact, profile, track or target any child outside the Company's approved processes; and
- (d) report any actual or suspected personal data breach or security incident to the Company within 24 hours.

## 7. Outside activities

7.1 During the engagement, the Employee shall:
- (a) not work for, advise or invest in any business that competes with the Company, except passive holdings of listed shares;
- (b) not use Company time, equipment or Confidential Information for outside work; and
- (c) tell the Company in writing before taking on any outside work that relates to the Company's business.

7.2 Nothing in this Agreement restricts the Employee from working in any trade or business after the engagement ends. Only the confidentiality and Intellectual Property obligations, and clause 8, continue after it ends.

## 8. Non-solicitation

For 12 months after the engagement ends, the Employee shall not solicit for employment or engagement any employee or contractor of the Company with whom they worked in their last 12 months. This does not stop general advertisements that are not targeted at them.

## 9. Leaving the Company

When the engagement ends, or earlier if the Company asks, the Employee shall within 3 days:
- (a) return all Company property, devices and documents;
- (b) hand over all work, accounts and credentials they hold for the Company;
- (c) delete any Confidential Information left on personal devices or accounts; and
- (d) confirm all of this in writing.

The Company will revoke the Employee's access on their last day.

## 10. Remedies

The Employee acknowledges that a breach of clauses 2 to 9 may cause the Company harm that money cannot adequately make good. In addition to any other remedy, the Company may seek an injunction and specific performance under the Specific Relief Act, 1963.

## 11. General

11.1 **Law and courts:** This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. The courts at Hyderabad, Telangana have jurisdiction.

11.2 **Inconsistency:** If this Agreement and the Appointment Letter conflict on confidentiality or Intellectual Property, this Agreement prevails.

11.3 **No waiver; severability:** A delay in enforcing a right is not a waiver of it. If any part is held unenforceable, the rest continues, and the unenforceable part is read down to the extent needed.

11.4 **Assignment:** The Company may assign this Agreement to any successor to the SportnNote business or to its affiliates.

11.5 **Survival:** This Agreement survives the end of the engagement, however it ends.
