## 1. Background

1.1 **SportnNote** is a multi-sport scoring, tournament-management and athlete-records platform, conceived and developed by [FOUNDER FULL NAME].

1.2 At the Founder's request, the Contributor made the following contribution to SportnNote between [START DATE] and [END DATE]: [DESCRIBE, e.g. "set up the initial code repository and Expo project (Git commit bec3655, 12 June 2026)"] (the "**Contribution**").

1.3 The Contributor made the Contribution for SportnNote, intending that it would belong to the Founder and the SportnNote business, and now confirms that in writing.

## 2. Confirmation and assignment

2.1 **Confirmation:** The Contributor confirms that the Contribution was made for, and belongs to, the Founder and the SportnNote business, and that the Contributor has no claim to any part of SportnNote.

2.2 **Assignment:**
- (a) In return for ₹[AMOUNT, e.g. 1,000] (Rupees [AMOUNT IN WORDS] only), the receipt of which the Contributor acknowledges by signing this Deed, the Contributor hereby assigns to the Assignee, absolutely, all right, title and interest the Contributor has or may have in the Contribution.
- (b) This covers all copyright (including in computer programmes), inventions, designs, trade marks, database rights and other Intellectual Property, with the right to sue for past infringement.
- (c) For each right, the assignment is worldwide and lasts for the full term of that right, in every form, medium and mode.
- (d) It does not lapse under section 19(4) of the Copyright Act, 1957 if not exercised within one year, and it is not limited by section 19(5) or 19(6).

2.3 **Moral rights:** The Contributor agrees, to the extent the law allows, not to assert any moral rights (including under section 57 of the Copyright Act, 1957) in the Contribution.

2.4 **Passing to the company:** Where the Assignee is the Founder, the Contributor agrees that the Founder may transfer these rights to any company the Founder forms to carry on the SportnNote business, without further consent.

## 3. Contributor's confirmations

The Contributor confirms that:
- (a) the Contribution was the Contributor's own work, apart from openly licensed templates or tools;
- (b) the Contributor did not make it in the course of any employment that would give an employer rights in it, and has not assigned or licensed it to anyone else;
- (c) the Contributor has no copies of SportnNote's code or confidential information, or will delete them on signing; and
- (d) the Contributor will keep any SportnNote information they know confidential.

## 4. Further assurance

At the Assignee's request and cost, the Contributor shall sign any further document reasonably needed to confirm or register the Assignee's rights.

## 5. Law

This Deed is governed by the laws of India. The courts at Hyderabad, Telangana have exclusive jurisdiction.
