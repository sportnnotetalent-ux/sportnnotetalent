# Wave 4 polish for the court-team family: break it first

2026-11-08. Wave 4 of docs/sport-expert/defaults-on/PLAN.md for volleyball, kabaddi and netball. It covers three items:

1. **Kabaddi K1.** After a quick "+1 touch", a non-blocking "Who was touched?" card (the shared `DescribeCard`, keyed by the raid's `eid`) names the touched defender afterwards. A new engine step, `RAID_TOUCHED`, does this. After it, "Defenders touched" (`trackTouched`) is on for a new match.
2. **Volleyball after full time.** The "Last point" card also shows after the match has ended, so the winning point can still get "Set by", "Who was blocked" or "Who was aced".
3. **Netball edit re-entry.** A goal or stat that is re-entered from "Correct the timeline" keeps its feeder, rebound, deflection outcome and turnover type, as hockey's `carry` does.

Everything below was listed after reading the code and **before** any fix, each with Status: Open. Each row then got a failing test in `tests/polish-court-team.test.mts`.

| # | What could go wrong | Kind | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| PC1 | **A quick "+1 touch" can't name the defender.** The one-tap raid never asks "Who was touched?", so a scorer who uses it can't record who went out. `trackTouched` therefore stays off for every new match (defaults-on-court-team DOC13). | Missing stats | **Fixed:** after a quick "+1 touch" (with "Defenders touched" on) a "👋 Who was touched?" `DescribeCard` is keyed by that raid's `eid`. It has Done / Skip and "✓ Saved · Touched X · Edit". Each tap dispatches `touchedAction(s, eid, defenders)`, which is one `RAID_TOUCHED {eid, touched: [{id}]}` step. Tested. |
| PC2 | **Naming after the fact gives a different match from naming in the form.** If the step re-entered the raid (a raid edit), it would bump `seq`, drop the raid's frozen `lp` and log a reversal + re-credit pair. The state would then differ from the same raid named in the full form. | Wrong state | **Fixed:** `RAID_TOUCHED` only sets the raid's `touched` in place (through `withRaids`). It does not change `seq`, `lp`, the score, the mat or the credits. The state, `kabaddiTotals`, `kabaddiDeepBox`, careers and commentary equal the full raid form with the defender named. Tested, and in the simulation. |
| PC3 | **A touch named on a raid that is gone** (undone, removed in the timeline, a legacy hidden raid) or an unknown `eid` credits a defender for nothing. | Wrong stats | **Fixed:** `touchedAction` returns null, and the reducer ignores a `RAID_TOUCHED` whose raid is missing or hidden. Tested. |
| PC4 | **A touch named on a raid with no touches**: an empty raid, a raider out without a tackle (line-out), or a raider out with 0 touches. | Wrong stats | **Fixed:** the same rule as the form (`raidActions`): only a raid with touches > 0 and no line-out. Anything else is ignored. Tested. |
| PC5 | **More names than touches, duplicate names, or names instead of ids** in a crafted payload. | Data / privacy | **Fixed:** the list is capped at the raid's touches, de-duplicated, and IDs only (a `name` entry is dropped, IDs-only scoring). An empty list clears the names. A payload that is not a list is ignored. Tested. |
| PC6 | **Undo of a describe tap**, or of the raid after it. | Undo | **Holds:** each tap is one step, so Undo removes only that tap and restores the previous names. Undoing the raid (after its taps) removes it, and the card closes because its raid is gone. The undo bar reads "who was touched". Tested in the simulation. |
| PC7 | **Old logs change.** | Replay | **Holds:** `RAID_TOUCHED` is a new action that no old log has. The `RAID_OUTCOME` case is not changed. Old logs replay to the states recorded on the code before this wave. Tested in the simulation against a fixed fingerprint. |
| PC8 | **The touch that ends the match** (a Golden Raid point, or an all out that ends it) can never be described, because the reducer drops every step once `ended`. | Missing stats | **Fixed:** `RAID_TOUCHED` is allowed after the end. It never changes the score or the result. Tested. |
| PC9 | **A late tap lands on the wrong raid.** | Wrong play | **Holds:** the step names its raid by `eid`, so a tap always lands on the raid it describes. The card shows only while its raid is the latest (`lastRaidEid`). Tested. |
| PC10 | **Zeros once "Defenders touched" is on by default.** A scorer who never names anyone must not get "Times raided out 0" or "Unsuccessful tackles 0" on every defender, or a career tackle success rising to 100%. | False zeros | **Holds (wave 1):** tracked means at least one named (`kabaddiTouchedTracked`). In the simulation, matches with nothing named carry no `touchedOut` / `unsuccessfulTackles` keys, and clearing the last name takes them away again. Tested. |
| PC11 | **The default flips for an old match.** | Replay | **Holds:** `init` adds `touched` only when the stored format carries `trackTouched: true`. Old formats (no key, or off) stay off. Tested. |
| PC12 | **Defaults drift, and the wording says "off".** | Clarity | **Fixed:** `KABADDI_STATS_DEFAULTS.trackTouched` is true, and the format fields and Standard read it. The settings note and plugin comment say what is on and that the card is optional. Tested. |
| PC13 | **Volleyball: the last point of the match can't be described.** Once `ended`, the detail row returns early with the switch only, so the "Last point" card is gone. The match-winning Attack, Block or Ace never gets its "Set by", "Who was blocked" or "Who was aced", although the engine accepts these (anchored kinds pass the `ended` check). | Missing stats | **Fixed:** after full time the row shows the "Last point" card for the last point (keyed by its event id, the same `pt` rule). It does not show the switch (`SET_DETAIL` is closed after the end) or the rally chips (in play, reception and dig are not accepted after the end). Tested (engine + source). |
| PC14 | **Volleyball after full time: a rally entry slips in** (dig, reception, in play), or a stale `pt` lands. | Wrong stats | **Holds:** the engine drops un-anchored kinds once ended, and a `pt` that isn't the last point. Tested. |
| PC15 | **Netball: an edited goal loses its feeder.** "✎ Edit" holds the removal of the original goal (with its feeder credit reversed) and re-enters it as a plain GOAL, so the feeder's goal assist and feed vanish from the stats. | Lost stats | **Fixed:** `carryOf(s, e)` reads the play's feeder, rebound, outcome and type when the edit opens. `withCarry` puts them back on the re-entered play, in the same shape the old flow logged: the feeder as the GOAL / MISS second credit, `gain` / `tt` in the STAT payload, and the rebound as the linked `STAT rebound` right after its miss. Tested. |
| PC16 | **Netball: an edited miss loses its rebound.** The rebound is removed with its miss (`removalActions`), and the re-entry never brings it back. | Lost stats | **Fixed:** the re-entered miss is followed by its rebound (`ref` = the new miss). Tested. |
| PC17 | **Netball: an edited deflection or turnover loses its outcome or type.** | Lost stats | **Fixed:** carried in the STAT payload when the kind is the same. Tested. |
| PC18 | **Netball: the new shooter is the old feeder** (a shooter can't feed her own goal). | Wrong stats | **Fixed:** the feeder is dropped in that case, and the card can name another. Tested. |
| PC19 | **Netball: the carry leaks** into the next live play after the edit is cancelled. | Wrong stats | **Fixed:** the carry lives with the edit (`editAt`). The flow's close effect clears both, and a live play never reads it. Tested (source). |
| PC20 | **Netball: credits double or vanish** across the edit (the removal reverses the old feeder or rebounder, and the re-entry credits them again). | Wrong stats | **Holds:** the removal's −1 and the re-entry's +1 net to the same live credit sums as a match where the play was never edited. Tested in the simulation. |

**Process.** The break list was written first. Then `tests/polish-court-team.test.mts` was written. Run on 69e9bbc6, 19 of its 24 tests failed. The 5 that passed were the old-log fingerprint (recorded there), the volleyball engine checks and the volleyball simulation, because the engine already accepted the detail; volleyball's gap was in the UI only. The fix followed, and all 24 now pass. Tests that pinned `trackTouched` off were updated on purpose, each marked `polish PCx`: `defaults-on-court-team.test.mts` (DOC2, DOC3, DOC4 / DOC6, DOC13) and `sim-defaults-on-court-team.test.mts` (the new-match `track`). The file ends with a 600-scorer simulation: kabaddi quick touches named later, never, renamed or undone; volleyball matches whose last point is described after full time; netball edits of goals, misses and stats. Totals, careers (stat totals) and live credit sums equal the full-form flow, and old logs replay identically.
