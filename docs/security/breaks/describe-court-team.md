# Describe cards for the court-team family (wave 2): break it first

2026-11-08. Wave 2 of docs/sport-expert/defaults-on/PLAN.md brings the founder's badminton pattern to volleyball, kabaddi and netball (handball's chip steps already finish on a required button, so they are left as they are). The pattern is one `DescribeCard` (src/components/DescribeCard.tsx) per last play: a "✓ summary" line, a full-width **Done** (**Skip** when nothing is picked), a greyed "✓ Saved · … Edit" after Done, and a fresh card on the next play. Follow-up steps never block scoring. The play goes on the board at the scorer's tap, and the detail is attached after, keyed by the play's own id. Volleyball merges "Set by? / Who was blocked? / Who was aced?" into one "Last point" card. Kabaddi's "Tackled by?" gets the Done finish and can be edited. Netball's feed, rebound, deflection-outcome and turnover-type steps become a non-blocking "Last goal / Last play" card that dispatches a new `DESCRIBE` action (src/sports/netball/engine.ts). Old logs keep the detail inside the original action and replay unchanged. The kabaddi raid form, the handball chip steps and the netball centre-pass row are not changed.

| # | Break | Kind | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| DC1 | Netball, feeds on: a goal is not on the board until "Who fed?" is answered. A slow or distracted scorer delays the score, and a closed step used to drop it (wave 0). | Play delayed / lost | The goal (or miss) is dispatched on the GS / GA tap. The feeder is attached after by `DESCRIBE {id, field: 'feed'}` from a "Last goal" card. Tested. |
| DC2 | Netball miss: the miss waits for two steps (feed, then rebound). | Play delayed | The miss is logged on the tap. The rebound is attached after (`field: 'rebound'`) as the same `stat rebound` event the old flow logged, placed right after its miss with `ref` = the miss. Tested. |
| DC3 | Netball deflection / turnover: the stat waits for Gain / No gain or the turnover type. | Play delayed | The stat is logged on the player pick. The outcome (`field: 'gain'`) and type (`field: 'tt'`) are attached after. Tested. |
| DC4 | Detail for a play that is gone (undone, removed in the timeline, voided by a correction) credits a feeder or rebounder for nothing. | Wrong stats | `describeAction` returns null when the id is not in the log, and the reducer ignores a `DESCRIBE` whose target is gone. Tested. |
| DC5 | Changing the feeder or rebounder (Edit) leaves the first one credited. | Wrong stats | One `DESCRIBE` carries `attribution` (+ the new player) and `attribution2` (− the old one), so the live credits move in one step and an undo moves them back. Tested. |
| DC6 | Undo of a detail tap, or of the play after its detail. | Undo | Each tap is one action, so Undo removes only that tap and restores the previous feeder / rebound / outcome. Undoing the play (once its detail taps are undone) removes it, and its card closes because the play is gone. Tested in the sim. |
| DC7 | Old logs (feeder in the GOAL / MISS `attribution2`, a rebound `STAT` right after its `MISS`, `gain` / `tt` in the `STAT` payload) must replay unchanged. | Replay | The reducer's GOAL / MISS / STAT cases are not changed. `DESCRIBE` is a new case that no old log has. Tested against fixed expected states. |
| DC8 | Stats, careers and commentary drift between the old flow and the new one for the same match. | Wrong stats | The new flow ends in the same events as the old one (same player, feeder, rebound, gain and type), so `netballTotals`, the live credit sums and `netballFeed` are identical. Tested on every simulated match. |
| DC9 | Two plays come quickly, and a describe tap meant for the first lands on the second. | Wrong play | Netball and kabaddi address the play by its id (`DESCRIBE.id`, the raid `eid`). Volleyball's `VB_DETAIL` now carries the point it describes (`pt: {set, at}`), and the reducer drops it when the last point has moved on. Old entries (no `pt`) keep the old rule. Tested. |
| DC10 | The card for an earlier play stays up after a newer play, and the scorer describes the wrong one. | Wrong play | Netball's card follows `lastDescribable` (the last play: goal, miss, stat, penalty, sanction; centre-pass, CPR, held time, subs and positions are skipped). Volleyball's follows the last scored point. Kabaddi's "Tackled by?" shows only while its raid is the latest raid. Tested. |
| DC11 | Voice scoring: "goal GS home" never asked for the feeder. | Missing stats | A voice goal is logged like a tapped one, so the "Last goal" card opens for it. Tested (the voice action is a plain GOAL with no feeder, and `lastDescribable` picks it). |
| DC12 | Netball's play-on (or golden-goal) winner ends the match on the goal, so the card can never attach its feeder. | Missing stats | `DESCRIBE` is allowed after the end (`ENDED_OK`), and the full-time panel shows the card for the last play. Tested. |
| DC13 | A timeline correction (✎ Edit) of a goal re-enters it: does the re-entered goal keep a blocking step, or lose the original? | Correction | The re-entry is logged on the tap at its original time (SD-114 holds the removal until then, and Cancel on the shooter step still keeps the original, SC7). The card then describes the re-entered play by its new id. Tested via the engine. |
| DC14 | Volleyball's three rows each have a small top-right "Skip", lose their skips on remount, and have no Done or saved state. | Clarity | One "Last point" `DescribeCard` keyed by the point's event id, with marks in the row (`useDescribeMarks`). It shows the rows that fit the point (Set by after an Attack point, Who was blocked after a Block, Who was aced after an Ace), plus "✓ Set by Priya", Done / Skip and "✓ Saved · … Edit". Tested (source). |
| DC15 | Kabaddi "Tackled by?": a pick commits and hides the row with no confirmation, and there is no way to change it. | Clarity | A `DescribeCard` keyed by the raid's `eid`: a full-width Skip / Done, "✓ Saved · Tackled by X · Edit", and Edit renames the tackler through the raid edit (`tacklerActions(..., { rename: true })`), which reverses the first tackler's points. Tested. |
| DC16 | Kabaddi rename to the same tackler, or rename a raid that is no longer a tackle, writes a needless edit. | Data | `tacklerActions` returns [] for the same tackler, a line-out or a missing raid. Tested. |
| DC17 | Screen readers can't tell a card is optional or what it describes. | A11y | The shared card's labels ("Describe the last play", "Done describing" / "Skip describing", "Edit the description"), and each chip names its player. Tested (source). |
