# Invasion describe cards: score on the tap, describe after — break it first

2026-11-08. Wave 2 of docs/sport-expert/defaults-on/PLAN.md brings the badminton pattern to football, hockey, ice hockey, rugby union and rugby league (docs/sport-expert/defaults-on/invasion.md, "Describe cards"). The goal or try goes on the board at the scorer's tap (or "Team goal / try"). The assist, second assist, strength, empty net and on-ice players are then described on a `DescribeCard` keyed by that goal's event id: the chips, a "✓ summary" line, a full-width **Done** (**Skip** when nothing is picked), a greyed "✓ Saved · … Edit", and a fresh card for the next goal. Each detail is its own logged step, `GOAL_DETAIL` (hockey, ice hockey) or `TRY_DETAIL` (union, league), with the ref of its goal. Football keeps its SD-114 `ASSIST` (with `ref`). The defining steps stay as they were: goal type, scorer, league's try zone, and ice hockey's strength picked before the scorer. The hockey penalty-corner card and football's stoppage reason get the same finish. The ice hockey faceoff zone does not; it is the tap that records the faceoff (see DI16). This supersedes wave 0's "Save as is" on those assist steps (score-on-close.md SC4–SC6): there is no describe step before the score any more.

| # | Break | Kind | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| DI1 | The score waits for the assist step. Closing it, or the app dying on it, loses or delays the goal. | Wrong score | Hockey, ice hockey, union and league fire GOAL / TRY on the scorer tap ("Team goal" too). The assist steps are gone from the flow. Tested by source checks and the 600-scorer sim. |
| DI2 | An assist attached later credits differently from the old in-flow assist: stats, careers, box score and commentary drift. | Wrong stats | The detail step's credits come from the engine's own `eventCredits`. They are the difference between the goal before and after (`creditDiff`), so GOAL + detail sums to exactly the old GOAL's credits. Its state equals the old one (no `seq` bump). Tested per sport, and statTotals equal the live sums. |
| DI3 | Changing the assister (A, then B) leaves A's assist on A's stat line. | Wrong stats | The change is one step: +1 to B and −1 to A (`attribution` / `attribution2`). Clearing (tap A again) is −1 to A. Football's ASSIST change carries A's −1 as `attribution2`. Tested. |
| DI4 | Undo of a goal whose assist came later: the assist row survives or the goal vanishes first. | Wrong score | Undo takes the newest step: first the assist (labelled "assist · B"), then the goal. Every prefix replays to a consistent state, and the live sums equal statTotals at each prefix. Tested in the sim. |
| DI5 | The assist lands on the wrong goal when two goals come quickly (it used to go to "the side's latest"). | Wrong stats | The detail step names its goal by id (`ref`), and the engine attaches it only there. The card is keyed by that id. A ref that is gone (Undo) does nothing. Tested. |
| DI6 | The card for an earlier goal is still open after a newer goal, so taps describe the wrong goal. | Wrong stats | One card, for the goal just scored. A newer goal replaces it, and its marks (Done / Skip) are per id, so the new card opens fresh. A goal removed by Undo or a correction hides its card. Tested by source checks. |
| DI7 | Voice scoring. | Data | Voice parsers still send one GOAL / TRY with any assist named in the same sentence (the old shape, on the board at once). Football's voice "who assisted?" attaches with the goal's `ref`. Unchanged and tested. |
| DI8 | Old logs (assist inside GOAL / TRY, ice hockey's ASSIST2) must replay unchanged. | Data | The GOAL / TRY / ASSIST2 reducers are untouched. The new step only adds a case. Tested: an old log equals a new log of the same match, and an old log replays as before. |
| DI9 | Correcting a goal from the timeline (✎ Edit) loses the assist: the re-entry fires at the scorer tap now. | Data | Hockey, ice hockey, union and league: the re-entered goal / try carries the original's assist(s) and on-ice players when they are still valid (not the new scorer), and its card lets you change them. Football: the fresh card offers the assist again, as its old assist panel did. While a correction is open, the card is hidden (it holds a removal). Tested by source check. |
| DI10 | Changing a football goal's type on the card (Header, Penalty…) re-logs the goal and drops its assist, but the assister's +1 stays live. | Wrong stats | The re-log reverses the assist with the goal (UNDO_GOAL's `attribution2`) and re-attaches it to the new goal. Tested. |
| DI11 | A sudden-death goal / try (ice hockey OT, union and league golden point) ends the match, and the engine then refuses the detail. | Data | `GOAL_DETAIL` / `TRY_DETAIL` are allowed after full time, like ice hockey's ASSIST2. The card also shows on the full-time screen. Tested. |
| DI12 | A hostile or stale detail: an assist on the scorer, a penalty-shot goal, a ref to another side's goal, or a junk strength. | Data | The engine ignores a detail whose ref is not a goal / try of that side, an assister equal to the scorer, an ice hockey penalty-shot goal's assists, and an unknown strength. Tested. |
| DI13 | Hockey's penalty-corner card: the only finish was a small "Later". | Clarity | `DescribeCard` keyed by the PC id: the outcome buttons, "✓ PC defended", Done / Skip, then "✓ Saved · … Edit". Source-checked. |
| DI14 | Football's stoppage reason: no Skip and no saved state. | Clarity | `DescribeCard` keyed by the stoppage id: the reason chips, Done / Skip, then "✓ Saved · Injury". Source-checked. |
| DI15 | Football's goal card finished only with "Close" at the top right. | Clarity | The goal card is a `DescribeCard` under the Goal buttons, keyed by the goal id. Its summary reads like "Header · Inside the box · Assist: X", with Done / Skip. Scoring carries on: the next Goal tap opens a fresh card. Source-checked. |
| DI16 | Ice hockey faceoff zone. | Not changed | Not a describe-after card. The zone tap (or "Skip the zone") is the tap that logs the faceoff and credits both centres, and nothing is described later. Turning it into an after-card needs a zone-attach step that re-credits both centres. That belongs with wave 3, when zones may default on. |
| DI17 | A post-match #05 void of the GOAL row leaves its detail row's credit. | Wrong stats | Hockey, ice hockey, union and league own every key in `statTotals` (absolute sync at completion and on corrections), so the lines follow the state. Football's assists are live increments, as before SD-114. Accepted and noted. |
