# No false zeros in volleyball, kabaddi, netball and handball — break it first

2026-11-08, wave 1 of `docs/sport-expert/defaults-on/PLAN.md` (specialist report `docs/sport-expert/defaults-on/court-team.md`, Risk R1). In these four sports, "was this stat tracked?" was decided by the "Stats to track" switch, or by the switch plus any logged entry. A scorer who left a stat switched on but skipped every prompt therefore got a 0 on every player's line, in Stats and in careers, and rates built on those zeros (tackle success near 100%, reception efficiency with no errors). Several of these switches are already on by default (volleyball `vbAssist` and `vbAced`, netball `trackCpr` in the International preset, both handball switches), and wave 3 will turn more on. The rule that racket sports already follow (`describe-done.md` DD11) applies here too: a stat counts as tracked only once at least one entry of that kind was captured in the match. Switched on with nothing captured means nothing shown, nothing written to careers and no rate computed. Where entries were captured, every figure is exactly what it was before, and old saved matches replay unchanged. Defaults and presets are not changed here (that is wave 3).
