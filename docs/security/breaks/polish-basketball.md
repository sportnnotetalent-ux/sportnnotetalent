# Basketball polish — "Rebound?" and "Steal — who lost it?" as describe cards (wave 4) — break it first

2026-11-08. Wave 2 (`describe-us.md`) moved basketball's assist / fast break, block and "who was fouled?" to the shared `DescribeCard` (`src/components/DescribeCard.tsx`). Two prompts were left on the old pattern: the "⏬ Rebound?" card after a miss (or a missed last free throw) and "✋ Steal — who lost it?". Each had a small header Skip link, and a tap logged the pick through `fire`, which closed the card at once: no summary, no Done, no way to fix a wrong pick. This wave moves both to `DescribeCard`, keyed by the miss / steal event: the full-width Done / Skip, "✓ Saved · Rebound: X · Edit", and single-choice chips (a re-tap clears, another player replaces).

Audit of "does either block scoring?": no. Both cards sit under the pads (outside the `flow` panel), and the play itself (the miss, the missed free throw, the steal) is on the board at its tap. The rebound and the ball-handler's turnover were always separate actions (`REBOUND`; `TURNOVER` with `ref`). So no new engine step is needed, the engine is untouched, and old logs replay identically by construction (PB7).
