## The serve in padel (FIP Rules of Padel; Premier Padel)

- Underarm: the server bounces the ball behind the service line and strikes it at or below the waist; it must land in the receiver's service box (diagonal).
- **Fault**: into the net; long (past the service line); wide (outside the box, the wrong box); a **foot fault** (stepping on / over the line, both feet off the ground, walking or running); and a serve that lands in the box and then **hits the wire mesh** (the metal fence) before its second bounce. The glass is fine; the mesh is not.
- **Let**: the serve touches the net (cord) and lands in the right box, or then hits the mesh — replayed, any number of times; no score effect, nothing to record.
- **Two serves**: a 2nd-serve fault is a **double fault**, the point to the receivers.

## Follow-on breaks to check while fixing (all checked: tests / simulation)
- An ace after a fault is a 2nd-serve ace (padel's Ace is a POINT with `pd: ace`).
- Doubles: the fault is the serving player's; the serve rotates through the pair (serve.ts), so after a game the other side serves and the old server can't fault.
- A code-violation **warning** isn't a point and must not clear the fault; a **point penalty** must.
- Time violations stay tennis-only (padel F13 waits for the FIP text): a padel CONDUCT with `track: 'time'` must not become a fault.
