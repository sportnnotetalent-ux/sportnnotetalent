# Describe cards in US sports (basketball, American football, baseball) — break it first

2026-11-08, wave 2 of `docs/sport-expert/defaults-on/PLAN.md` for the US family (`docs/sport-expert/defaults-on/us-sports.md` items 2/3/7, US-DON-3/4/5). Every "describe the last play" step becomes the shared `DescribeCard` (`src/components/DescribeCard.tsx`, `docs/security/breaks/describe-card.md`): the sport's chips, a "✓ summary" line, a full-width Done (Skip when nothing is picked), a greyed "✓ Saved · … Edit", and a fresh card on the next play. Follow-ups never block scoring: the play goes on the board at the scorer's tap, and the detail is attached after by its own action, keyed to that play's id (football SD-114's `GOAL_DETAIL` model). Basketball: the "Assist? / ⚡ Fast break" card and the block card move to `DescribeCard`; a foul is logged at its type tap and "who was fouled?" becomes an optional card (`FOUL_DRAWN`). American football: an incompletion logs at the ✗ tap and its intended receiver is attached after (`TARGET`); "intended for", "broken up by", "fumble forced by" and "tackle by" sit in one non-blocking card under the pads. Baseball: "Fielders?" gets the Done finish. Old logs, with the detail inside the original action, replay unchanged, and an equivalent new sequence gives the same events, stats, careers, box scores and commentary. Wave 1's "tracked = captured" (`no-false-zeros-us.md`) stays true.

| # | Break | Kind | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| DU1 | A play is lost or delayed: basketball's foul isn't logged until "who was fouled?" is answered, and American football's incompletion waits for "intended for". Cancel there drops the foul / the pass, and the clock, team fouls and bonus lag behind. | Data | The foul is logged at the type tap (team fouls, bonus, foul-out and free throws as before); the incompletion at the ✗ tap (or the passer pick). The detail comes after. Tested. |
| DU2 | The follow-up hides the pads: American football's target / breakup / forced fumble / tackle grids replace the scoring buttons until answered. | UX | Those grids are gone from the blocking flow. One `DescribeCard` under the pads holds them; the next play closes it. Tested (source). |
| DU3 | Undo of a play whose detail was attached later leaves a phantom detail or a card for a play that is gone. | Data | Undo removes the newest action first: the detail, then the play. A detail action for a play that is gone is ignored by the engine, and the card closes when its play leaves the log. Tested. |
| DU4 | A detail action names the wrong kind of play or carries junk (stale client, hostile payload): `FOUL_DRAWN` on a basket, a technical or a coach technical; `TARGET` on a run, a completion or a play wiped out by a penalty; bad ids. | Security | The engines check the target's id and kind and the value's type; anything else changes nothing. Tested. |
| DU5 | Correction / edit: the timeline Edit of a foul or an incompletion forgets the detail attached later, or Remove leaves its credit behind. | Data | The detail lives on the event itself (`drawnPid`, `secondId` + `tgt`), so Edit keeps it and Remove reverses it with the event's own credits. Tested. |
| DU6 | Old logs (detail inside the original action: `FOUL` with `drawnPid`, `PLAY inc` with the receiver and `tgt`, fielders inside the play) replay differently. | Replay | The old actions are untouched and still read. The 600-scorer sim replays both shapes. Tested. |
| DU7 | The new two-step flow gives different numbers: stats, careers (`statTotals`), box score, commentary or the panel differ from the one-step flow for the same game. | Stats | The detail actions push no event and don't move `seq`, so the event list (ids included) is the same. The sim compares every output. Tested. |
| DU8 | Two plays come quickly (or by voice), and the detail lands on the wrong one: the ⚡ chip tags "the side's latest basket", not the one the card is about. | Data | Every detail names its play by id: `TAG` with the basket's `id`, `ASSIST` / block shooter with `ref`, `FOUL_DRAWN` / `TARGET` with the play's id, baseball `FIELDERS` with `ref`. Tested. |
| DU9 | The card for an earlier play is still open after a newer play went on the board (a voice play, or a play from another tap path), so the next tap describes the wrong play. | Data | Each card checks the log: once a newer play that isn't its own detail is logged, it closes (`cardStale`, `afCardStale`, `fieldersStale`). Tested. |
| DU10 | Voice scoring: voice plays bypass the controls, so they neither close an open card nor attach detail; a voice foul / incompletion must still score at once. | Voice | Voice actions are unchanged (they were already one-shot). A voice play closes the open card through DU9. Tested. |
| DU11 | Fouled-out or ejected players get credit through a late detail ("fouled by", assist, block shooter, AF tackler). | Rules | The chips leave out players who are out (as before). The engine drops `FOUL_DRAWN` naming a player who is out. AF lists skip ejected players. Tested. |
| DU12 | A shooting foul's free throws: before, picking who was fouled pre-picked the shooter (net zero taps). Moving the pick after the foul could cost a tap, or the free throws could close the card. | UX | The card shows above the free-throw panel during that trip. Picking who was fouled also picks the shooter until the first shot, and the trip's own free throws don't close the card. Tested (source). |
| DU13 | Changing your mind gives two credits for one play: a second assist on a basket, two shooters on one block, two tacklers, two targets. | Data | The card's chips are single-choice: a re-tap of the chosen player clears it, and another player replaces it (the old credit is removed first). Tested. |
| DU14 | Skip or Done reads wrong: the fast-break card still says "Skip" after a tag (looks like it throws the tag away); nothing shows what was saved. | UX | `DescribeCard`: Done once anything is picked, Skip when nothing is. The summary is read from the log ("Assist: Ben · Fast break"), and Done greys it to "✓ Saved · …" with Edit. Tested. |
| DU15 | Wave 1 broken: a skipped card writes a false zero (fouls drawn, targets, passes defended, tackles). | Stats | Nothing is captured until a player is named: `fdt` still rides on the foul, but `foulsDrawnOn` needs a name; AF columns need a credited event. Tested in the sim. |
| DU16 | Undo labels: undoing a late detail says just "Undo", or names the play it isn't removing. | UX | Undo names it: "who drew the foul", "intended receiver". Baseball's fielders note is a correction already. Tested. |
| DU17 | Re-entering a past play (timeline Edit / backfill) opens a describe card stamped now, for a play in the past. | Data | Edits and corrections never open a card (as before for assists and fielders). Tested (source). |
