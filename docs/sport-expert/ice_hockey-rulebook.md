## Result

The IIHF preset matches Rule 84:
- round robin: 5-minute sudden-death overtime, 3 on 3, then a shoot-out (5 different shooters each, alternating, then tie-break shots; only the deciding goal counts);
- play-off: 10-minute overtime, 3 on 3;
- standings use regulation wins and ROW.

The NHL, US high school and youth presets keep their own rules.
