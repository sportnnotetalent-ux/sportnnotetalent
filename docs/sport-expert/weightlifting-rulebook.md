## Result

The categories match the **8 May 2025** revision:
- Senior / junior men: 60, 65, 71, 79, 88, **94**, 110, +110.
- Senior / junior women: 48, 53, 58, 63, 69, 77, 86, +86.
- Youth men: 56 … **94**, +94.
- Youth women: 44 … 77, +77.

Note: the December 2024 announcement said 98 kg; the May 2025 revision (after an IOC decision) replaced it with 94 kg. Our code follows the revision. A first read of only the older page would have "fixed" this wrongly.

No findings.
