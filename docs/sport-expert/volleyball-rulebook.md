## Checked and fine

- Sets to 25 (5th to 15), two clear, no cap. Best of 5 / 3. ✓
- Rotation clockwise on winning the serve back; libero replacement; 6 substitutions per set. ✓
- Two time-outs per set; change of ends at 8 in the deciding set. ✓
- Fault types: net, centre line, double / 4 hits, catch, back-row / libero, block fault. ✓
