## Findings

| # | Priority | Law | Gap | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FB-1 | **P1** | Law 3.1–3.2: in 11-a-side competitions, up to 5 substitutes; return ("rolling") substitutions only in grassroots, youth, veterans and disability football | The 11-a-side preset (the tournament default) used rolling substitutions, so a substituted player could come back on. | **Fixed:** 11-a-side uses fixed substitutions (no return). 7s, 5s and futsal stay rolling. Organisers can still change it. |
| FB-2 | P2 | Law 3.2: at most 3 substitution opportunities in play (plus half-time; one more in extra time) | Not enforced. Only the count of 5 is. | To build: an optional "3 substitution windows" check that counts stoppages. |
| FB-3 | P3 | Law 12 (8-second rule) / 16 (2026/27: delaying a goal kick): the restart is a **corner** | A corner is recorded without its reason. | Optional reason chip later. |
| FB-4 | P3 | Law 10.3 (2026/27): an accidental double touch in a shoot-out; no automatic caution when kicker and keeper both offend | Shoot-out kicks are scored / missed / retaken by hand. | Nothing to change. A retake is already possible. |
| FB-5 | P3 | Law 3 (2026/27): up to 8 substitutes (11 by agreement) in senior international friendlies; a substituted player must leave within 10 seconds | Substitutes go up to 11 in the format. | Nothing to change. |

## Checked and fine

- 2 × 45 minutes, extra time 2 × 15, then kicks from the penalty mark (ABBA not used). ✓
- Cards and second yellow → red; concussion substitutes outside the limit (max 2). ✓
- Futsal: stopped clock, time-outs, accumulated fouls (6th → 10 m kick). ✓
