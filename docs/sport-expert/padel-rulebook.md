## Checked and fine

- Rule 1: advantage, Star Point (deuce 1 → advantage → deuce 2 → advantage → deuce 3 → star point) and golden point. ✓
- Sets of 6 with a tie-break to 7 (two clear) at 6-6; ends change every 6 tie-break points; the next set is served by the pair that didn't serve first in the tie-break. ✓
- Alternative formats: 4-game mini sets, super tie-break to 10 replacing the last set. ✓
- Serve: underhand, at or below the waist, after a bounce in the box, second serve after a fault; double fault = point (SD-117c). ✓
- Rule 9: lets (receiver not ready) as a marker; a serve from the wrong side keeps the points scored. ✓
