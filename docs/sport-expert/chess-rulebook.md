## Result

The 2023 Laws are still current. The 2026 regulation updates concern ratings, titles and administration, not results. Results (1-0, ½-½, 0-1), forfeits (FIDE C.07 unplayed games), byes, and the tie-break family (Sonneborn-Berger, Buchholz and cuts, direct encounter, wins, games with Black) are built (SD-10 / SD-26 / SD-67).

No findings.
