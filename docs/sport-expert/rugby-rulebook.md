## Result

Already built:
- the 20-minute red (elite rugby): the player stays off, and a replacement may come on after 20 minutes of playing time;
- two yellows = a 20-minute red, unless the second offence is a full red;
- a full red for intentional, highly dangerous play (no replacement).

It's an organiser option, and the discipline table counts 20-minute reds separately.
