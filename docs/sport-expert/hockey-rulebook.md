# Field hockey: rulebook audit (2026-11-05)

Tenth sport. It builds on `invasion.md` (cards, green / yellow timings, shoot-outs, team officials).

## Source

| Document | Version | Link |
|---|---|---|
| FIH Rules of Hockey, including explanations | Effective 1 Nov 2026 (international), 76 pp. | https://www.fih.hockey/static-assets/pdf/2026_09_14_fih_rules_of_hockey_2027_clean.pdf |

FIH: "no core elements of the game are being changed", and no major changes before LA 2028.

## Findings

| # | Priority | Rule (2026) | Gap | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| HK-1 | P2 | Rule 14: the same player or official must not get two green or two yellow cards in a match. The next card must be a higher category (cards for a team offence don't count). | We already warned on a second green or yellow, but called a red after a yellow only "usually". | **Fixed:** the warning cites Rule 14 (2026) and says a further card after a yellow must be red. It stays a warning: we record what the umpire shows. |
| HK-2 | P3 | Rule 2.3: a goalkeeper substitution no longer needs time stopped; a field player may replace the keeper | Substitutions and keeper changes are already free-form. | ✓ |
| HK-3 | P3 | Rule 13.3(a): about 40 seconds from the award to the start of a penalty corner (a countdown in regulations) | Not timed. | Leave. |
| HK-4 | P3 | Rule 13.5: a bully now completes a penalty corner (no retake) | Penalty corners are counted, not their completion. | ✓ |

## Checked and fine

- 4 × 15 minutes; goals from the field, penalty corners and strokes; shoot-outs (8 seconds, 5 each, then sudden death). ✓
- Green 2′, yellow 5–10′, red permanent; the clock stops a green card's time too. ✓
