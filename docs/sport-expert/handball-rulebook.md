## Result

The 2025 amendments don't touch scoring:
- the throw-off whistle only once the ball and thrower are inside the throw-off area;
- 3 steps counted from the first floor contact after catching in the air;
- 7:10, no playing the ball while touching the floor outside the court.

The 1 Jul 2026 updates are for beach and wheelchair handball only.
