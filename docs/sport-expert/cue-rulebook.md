## Result

- **Pool:** WPA published a new Rules of Play edition on 21 Oct 2026. The PDF has no change list. Its sections match what we score: legal break, push out after the break (9-ball / 10-ball), three consecutive fouls (`threeFoul`, a rack lost), playing out of turn, ball off the table, and the foul types.
- **Snooker / billiards:** WPBSA rules are unchanged since the build.
- **Carrom:** ICF rules are unchanged since the build.
