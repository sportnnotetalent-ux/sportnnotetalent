The founder wants a digital-marketing agent team promoting SportnNote on three platforms:
- **LinkedIn:** announcements before, during and after launch, and major updates.
- **Instagram:** value, updates, quick how-tos, customer usage and testimonials.
- **YouTube:** how-to videos.

The plan lives in the claude.ai doc "SportnNote social launch roadmap" (https://claude.ai/artifact/2iJU3vo85PXYAqKJuxXfYP; doc/project id 0ddd65b3-bb12-4401-8a6c-db30e8638b54).

**Founder decisions (2026-10-11):**
- **Timeline:** October–November is for app launch readiness (accounts, brand kit, AI presenter, recorded backlog; nothing public). Pre-launch posting runs 7 Dec – 10 Jan. Public launch is Mon 11 Jan 2027 at 10:00 IST. The first season runs to 14 Mar.
- **Handles:** none exist yet. Create @sportnnote first; fallbacks are @sportnnote.app, @sportnnoteapp, @sportnnote_in.
- **"Free":** allowed, always with the line "free while we're new; a small fee later, announced well in advance" (30 days' notice).
- **On camera:** AI-arranged screens and an AI presenter for product videos, disclosed per platform AI labels. The founder appears for the LinkedIn launch video and major announcements.

**How to apply:**
- Never use AI faces as users, students or testimonials.
- Minors appear only with written guardian consent plus school permission, and never full name + school + face together.
- No competitor names.
- Release posts are drafted from DEVLOG + `website/guides` and need founder approval before posting.

Related: [[sportfolio-feature-guides]], [[sportfolio-sport-equality]].

**Standing ask (2026-10-11):** content must keep flowing in parallel with the build. After each feature batch, run a content agent that writes ready-to-post copy, full video transcripts, dates and platforms into `docs/marketing/` (content-calendar.md + posts/) and refreshes the roadmap doc's "Content calendar (live)" section. Every shipped change that users would notice also gets a new or updated guide on sportnnote.in/guides.
