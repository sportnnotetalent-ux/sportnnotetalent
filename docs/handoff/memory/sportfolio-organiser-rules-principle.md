When a rule detail varies by competition or can't be confirmed from a rulebook (walkover scores, ban lengths, lifting order, categories, athletics/XC/combined-event factors, shoot-out order), don't hard-code it: give the tournament organiser an option with a standard default, set before the tournament starts (locked once results exist). Friendlies use the federation default.

**Why:** the founder doesn't want the app to be wrong on rules it can't verify; school organisers run their own regulations. They also said golf should use standard options until user feedback says otherwise.

**How to apply:**
- Walkover goals/points count to the team (standings, team stats, team pages) and never to any individual player.
- Team stats show walkovers won and given.
- Scorers choose who goes first in shoot-outs (toss).
- Medal places stay hidden for groups+knockout sports until the knockout starts.
- Groups keep live and historical standings, and knockouts keep history.
