An under-14 event only works if every player really is under 14. Each tournament has a **Players’ age** setting: leave it off, or ask for every player's age to be checked before they can be picked for a match. Schools and clubs that SportnNote has verified can confirm their own members' dates of birth in one go, so players don't have to upload documents.

## Before you start
- To set the rule, you must be a host of the tournament (hosts see **⚙ Manage**).
- To confirm members' dates of birth, you must be an Owner or Admin of the school or club, and SportnNote must have verified it first (see below).

## Step by step: set the rule
1. Open the tournament and tap **⚙ Manage**.
2. Tap **Edit details**.
3. Scroll down to **Players’ age**.
4. Tap **Not required**, **Confirmed** or **Verified**. It saves as soon as you tap it and shows "✓ Saved". You don't need **Save changes** for this one.

## What each option means
- **Not required**: the default. Age groups still use the date of birth players entered.
- **Confirmed**: every player's age must be confirmed by a parent, their school or club, or a document checked by SportnNote's reviewers.
- **Verified**: only their school or club, or a reviewed document, counts. A parent's confirmation isn't enough.

The rule covers every player, adults too. Adults can get theirs checked by their school or club, or with a document.

## What changes for who can be picked
The rule is checked wherever players are picked for this tournament's matches:
- In the match squad, a player who doesn't meet it shows 🔒 and the reason, for example "Age not confirmed — this tournament needs a parent, their school / club, or a document to confirm it". They show **Not eligible** instead of **Start** and **Bench**.
- In the cricket line-up, their name shows with 🔒 and they can't be added.
- On the scoring screen, they're left out of the player lists, with a note saying how many are hidden and why.

Friendly matches that aren't part of a tournament never have the rule.

> **Note:** The rule applies to matches. It doesn't check entries to meet events such as athletics or swimming, or to golf rounds.

> **Tip:** Set the rule before teams pick their squads, and tell teams early. Players can follow [Get your age verified](/guides/verify-your-age/) to see what they need.

## Schools and clubs: confirm a whole squad at once
The fastest way to get a school's players through the rule is for the school to confirm their dates of birth itself. SportnNote verifies the school once. After that, its Owners and Admins can confirm members from the school's own records.

### Get your school or club verified
A school or college gets verified in three steps: its official ID, a recognition document, and proof that you're authorised by the school. See [Verify your school on SportnNote](/guides/verify-your-school/). Other registered organisations, such as clubs, tap **Ask SportnNote to verify us** on the **🛡️ Verification** card instead.

Renaming the school or club, or changing its type or official ID, removes its verification, so it needs verifying again.

### Confirm members' dates of birth
1. On the **Details** tab, tap **📋 Confirm members’ dates of birth**.
2. Check each date against your admission register or school ID records. Tick the members whose date matches, or tap **Select all**.
3. Tap the button at the bottom, for example **☑️ Confirm 8 dates of birth**.

Only current members of your school or club are listed. Members with no date of birth appear under "No date of birth yet": ask them to add it in **✎ Edit profile**. Members you've already confirmed appear under "Confirmed by you".

If a date is wrong, don't tick it. Ask the player to fix it in **✎ Edit profile**, then confirm it.

A school or club confirmation meets both **Confirmed** and **Verified**. The player's checklist then shows "Age verified by your school / club".

> **Important:** A confirmation counts only for the date of birth you confirmed. If a player changes their date of birth, they move back to the list to confirm.

## Common questions
### Can I change the rule after matches have started?
Yes. From then on it applies to every match in the tournament. A player already in a squad who doesn't meet the new rule is left out of the scorer's player list, so it's best to set it before squads are picked.

### Does this replace age groups?
No. Age groups still use each player's date of birth. This setting decides whether that date must have been checked.

### Why doesn't a parent's confirmation meet "Verified"?
"Verified" is the stricter level: it accepts only the school or club's records or a document checked by SportnNote's reviewers. If a parent's word is enough for your event, choose **Confirmed**.
