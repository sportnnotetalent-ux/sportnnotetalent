Every athletics, swimming, rowing, canoe sprint and cycling meet gets a live commentary and a race analysis from the results you already enter. You can also choose to record more detail, such as lap times or relay legs. Extra detail is off unless you switch it on, so entering a result stays as quick as before.

## What everyone gets, with no extra entry
- **📣 Latest results** on the sport's page in the tournament: a feed of short lines, newest first. For example, "100 m U14 Girls · Heat 2: Priya Sharma wins in 12.48 Q; Asha Rao 12.61 Q (wind +0.8)", "Long jump U14 Girls · round 3: Asha Rao 5.42 (+0.4) — takes the lead", "High jump U16 Boys: Ravi Kumar clears 1.75 at the second attempt (XO)". Tap a line to open that event.
- **📣 Commentary** on each event's results sheet: every heat, trial and bar height of that event, the qualifiers, the medals and any record broken.
- **🪜 Through the rounds**: each athlete's path, for example "H2 12.61 (2) Q → SF1 12.50 (3) q → F 12.48 (1)".
- **Behind the winner**: each placed athlete's gap, in seconds for a race or metres for a jump or throw.
- **Profiles**: heats and semi-finals won now count, next to finals and medals. In a timed final (one final run in several heats), the winner of each heat counts too. A meet or school record an athlete breaks shows under **🏆 Records set**.
- **Alerts**: people who follow an athlete get a notification when the athlete wins a heat (their **Result** switch) or sets a personal best (their **big moments** switch), as well as the final's result.

The commentary is worked out from the results each time you open it. If an official corrects a time, the line changes too.

## Step by step: choose what to track
1. Open the tournament, then the sport's page (for example **Manage** → **Athletics — events**).
2. Tap **⚙ Points & timing** (swimming: **⚙ Pool, points & timing**; rowing and canoe: **⚙ Lanes, points & timing**).
3. Under **STATS TO TRACK**, set each one to **On** or **Off**:
   - **Athletics:** **Reaction times**, **Lap times** (races of 400 m and longer), **Lap points**, **Relay leg times** and **Chip times (road)**. **Lap points** sets where lap times are read: **Finish-line passes** (one timekeeper) or **Every 400 m from the start** (as in the Diamond League).
   - **Swimming:** **50 m splits** (on, as before), **Reaction times** and **Relay take-over times**.
   - **Rowing and canoe sprint:** **500 m splits** / **Intermediate times**.
   - **Cycling:** **Intermediate times** (pursuit every 1000 m, kilo and 500 m every lap, team sprint each rider's lap, and one halfway time check in a road time trial of 10 km or more).
4. Tap **Save**. You can change these during the meet: they only add boxes to the entry screen. They don't change any result.

### Just one event
To track something for one event only (for example lap times in the 1500 m), open the event, tap **Enter results**, then **⚙ Stats to track — this event**. Set each one to **On** or **Off**. Picking the meet's setting again makes the event follow the meet. You can also set this when you add a track or swimming event.

## Step by step: enter the extra detail
1. Open an event and tap **Enter results**.
2. **Lap times:** tap **＋ Splits** under a runner and type the time at each point, for example 5520 for 55.20. In a 1500 m the points are 300 m, 700 m and 1100 m (the finish line), or 400 m, 800 m and 1200 m with **Every 400 m from the start**. Times already typed keep their points if you change this.
3. **Relay legs:** on a relay team, tap **＋ Splits** and type the time at each changeover (100 m, 200 m and 300 m in a 4 × 100 m). Each runner's leg time follows.
4. **Take-overs (swimming relays):** type the pad reading for legs 2 to 4, such as 0.21 or −0.05. A reading earlier than −0.03 s is flagged ⚠ for the take-off judge. The app never disqualifies on its own: if the judge confirms, tap **DQ** → **SW 10.13 · Early take-off**.
5. **Chip times (road):** under each finisher's gun time, type the chip time. The gun time stays official and decides the places.
6. **DQ reasons (athletics):** after tapping **DQ**, pick the reason, such as **TR 17.3 · Lane infringement**. You can still type any other rule.

> **Tip:** Leave a box empty if you didn't get a time. The app never shows a missing time as zero.

## What the race analysis shows
Open an event and tap **Results sheet**. Under the sheet, **📈 Race analysis** shows only what was recorded:
- each athlete's split or lap times, with the lap in brackets and their place at each point;
- how the winner raced: "led at every split", "came from 3rd at 1100 m";
- the fastest last lap, length or 500 m;
- relay legs, the fastest flying leg and swimming take-overs;
- reaction times: the fastest, the average, and any under 0.100 s in athletics.

The results sheet also shows each athlete's reaction time (RT) when it was read.

## Relay lead-off times in swimming
With splits recorded, the first swimmer of a relay gets that time as an individual swim on their profile. A 4 × 100 m freestyle lead-off counts as a 100 m freestyle, and it can be a PB. Mixed relays never count. To turn this off, open **📜 Competition rules** → **Records** → **Relay lead-off times** → **Relay only** before the meet starts.

## Common questions
### Do old results change?
No. Nothing new is shown for an event that has no extra detail. The commentary simply reads the results you already entered.

### Can I switch tracking on halfway through a meet?
Yes. The new boxes appear on every event straight away. Events you've already entered just won't have the extra detail.

### Who can see the commentary?
Anyone who can see the meet's results, including people you share the results link with.
