Pool is scored rack by rack, to WPA rules. Tap every shot for the commentary and full stats, or just who won each rack.

## Before you start
- You must be a scorer of the match, or a host of the match or its tournament. See [Scorers and officials](/guides/scorers-and-officials/).
- For a friendly, tap **🤝 Start a friendly** on **Organize** (or in the **▾** menu on Home). Pick **🎱 Pool**, then **👤 Singles** or **👥 Scotch doubles** (partners take turns, shot by shot), the players and the rules. Tap **▶ Create & score now**.
- Under **🎱 Pool rules** the organiser picks the **Game** (fixed once play starts), **Race to**, **Sets**, **Who breaks** (**Alternate (WPA)**, **Winner breaks** or **Loser breaks**) and **Three fouls in a row lose the rack** (standard: on for 9- and 10-ball, off for 8-ball).

## Step by step
1. Open the match → **Scoring** tab and tap **▶ Start the match**.
2. Under **Who breaks rack 1? (the lag winner chooses)**, tap the player who breaks.
3. Tap each ball that went in on the break, then **✓ Break · the 3 and 7 in**. If nothing went in, tap **Dry break (nothing in)**.
4. For each shot, tap the balls that went in, then **Pot · the 1**. The game ball is marked ★, for example **9 ★**. Nothing in? Tap **Miss**. A safety? Tap **Safety**. In doubles, check who is shooting under **Shooting:** (the app picks whose turn it is).
5. The app passes the turn. Before a tap that wins a rack, set or match, it asks, for example "Rack 3 to Asha?". Tap **Yes, record it**.

At 8-ball, tap the called ball first: it sets the groups ("Asha: solids (1–7) · Ravi: stripes (9–15)"). At 8- and 10-ball, if the ball or pocket wasn't the one called, tap **Not called (wrong pocket / ball)** before **Pot**.

The break also has **Foul on the break ▸**, **Illegal break** and, at 9-ball, **Short break (three-ball rule)**. When a rule gives a player a choice, the screen asks it, for example "Short break (three-ball rule). Ravi chooses:", with a button for each answer.

## Fouls
1. Tap any balls that went in on the foul.
2. Tap **Foul ▸**, then the kind, such as **Cue ball pocketed or off the table**. Then tap the red **Record foul · …** button. Tapping a kind only picks it, so a stray tap records nothing.

The other player then has ball in hand. If the wrong player shot, tap **Foul ▸** → **Ravi played out of turn** (the button names them).

With the three-foul rule on, a player on two fouls in a row gets a banner: "⚠️ Ravi is on two fouls. A third foul in a row loses the rack." A legal shot clears the count.

## Push out at 9-ball and 10-ball
On the first shot after a legal break, the player may push out: a shot that needn't hit the lowest ball or a rail. Tap **Push out**, then the other player's choice: **Ravi plays from here** or **Ravi hands it back to Asha**.

## Quick mode: rack results only
Before a rack's break, under **Rack result only**, tap **Rack to Asha** or **Rack to Ravi**. First you can tap **Break-and-run**, **Golden break** (9-ball) or **Run-out (not the breaker)**. You can mix quick and shot-by-shot racks. Quick racks count for racks won, not pots or fouls.

If a rack can't be finished, tap **More: stalemate, award the rack ▸** to re-rack with the same breaker or award the rack. Both ask first.

## Fix a mistake
- Tap **↶ Undo**. The box beside it names what it takes back, for example "Last step: pot 3, 5 · Asha".
- The game, race and sets are fixed once the first rack starts, so check them on the match card first. Set up wrong? In the first 5 minutes, tap **↺ Restart match**. See [End a match early or record a walkover](/guides/end-a-match-early/).
- After the match, you can remove a shot or, in doubles, credit it to the other partner. See [Correct a match after full time](/guides/correct-a-finished-match/).

## The commentary and the stats
- **Commentary** tells each rack shot by shot, newest first: "Foul by Ravi: scratch · Asha to shoot with ball in hand".
- **Stats** compares the players for the match or one set: racks won, break-and-runs, balls on the break, pot success, safeties, innings and fouls by kind. The **Racks** list reads like "Rack 3 · Asha broke · Ravi won · run-out".

A break-and-run is a rack won by the breaker before the other player shoots; a run-out, one won in one visit by the player who didn't break. A golden break is the 9 going in on a legal break.

## Records and profiles
A finished match saves its records: biggest wins, break-and-runs, run-outs, golden breaks and most racks in a row. See [See a tournament's records](/guides/tournament-records/). Profiles add racks won, break-and-runs and, from shot-by-shot racks, pots and fouls.

## Common questions
### The 10 went in but the rack didn't end. Why?
Under the 2026 WPA rules the 10 wins only as the last ball; earlier it is spotted. The organiser can switch on **10-ball: an early called 10 wins**.
