Every SportnNote account for someone under 18 needs a parent or guardian's approval, in every country. The parent approves from their own SportnNote account by sending a photo ID and a document showing they are the parent or legal guardian. SportnNote checks them within 2 days. This way nobody else can approve in your name, not even your child with a second email address.

## Before you start
- The child signs up with their own details and their parent or guardian's name and email.
- You need your own SportnNote account, aged 18 or over. If you don't have one, you can create it from the link in a minute.
- Have two things ready, as a phone photo or a PDF of up to 10 MB each:
  - your **photo ID**: Aadhaar (masked), passport, driving licence, voter ID or PAN card;
  - a **document showing you're the parent or legal guardian**: your child's birth certificate naming you, a school record or ID card naming you as parent, your child's passport, or a court guardianship order.
- In the US, children under 13 can't create their own account. A parent adds them as a player from the parent's own account instead.

## Step by step: approving your child's account
1. Your child signs up and gives your email as their parent or guardian. SportnNote emails you a link. It works for 7 days.
2. Open the link. It shows your child's first name and age, and what SportnNote keeps and who sees it.
3. Tap **Sign in**, or **Create an account** if you don't have one. You come back to the same page.
4. Choose how you're related: **Mother**, **Father** or **Legal guardian**. Then type your name exactly as it is on your ID.
5. Add your photo ID and the relationship document. If it asks whether your child's date of birth is right, tap **✓ Yes, that’s right** or **No, it’s wrong**.
6. Tap **Send for checking**. SportnNote checks the documents within 2 days, and you and your child are told the result.

Once approved, your account is linked as your child's parent or guardian. Messages about your child come to you, and their date of birth counts as confirmed for events with age groups.

If the documents aren't accepted, you're told why, for example "the photo ID is hard to read". Open **Settings → Parental approvals** and tap **Send new documents**. You don't need a new link.

> **Tip:** Your child sees where things stand on their Home screen. If the email didn't arrive, they can tap **Send the link again**, up to 3 times a day. If the address was wrong, they correct it first in **Edit profile**.

## Until you approve
Your child can still play, be added to teams, be scored and see their own stats. Until you're approved:
- they don't appear in search, Discover, leaderboards or a school's Hall of Fame;
- they get no certificates, career passport or share link;
- nobody can follow them, and nobody can send messages about them.

Even after approval, people outside your child's teams, clubs and tournaments see only their initials, not their full name or age.

If you tap **I don’t agree** instead, the account is locked and deleted after 30 days, unless you approve later from a new link.

## What happens to your documents
- Only SportnNote's reviewers see them, inside the app. They never travel by email.
- They're deleted 30 days after the check, whether it was accepted or not.
- We never keep ID numbers; only the pictures, and only until then.

## Step by step: managing your child's data
1. Sign in with **your own** account, not your child's.
2. Go to **Settings → Link as a parent/guardian**.
3. Next to your child's name, tap **Manage**.
4. Choose what you need from the list below.

The **Manage** page has:
- **👁️ View**, a summary of what we keep;
- **⬇️ Download**, the full file (JSON);
- **✓ Yes, confirm their date of birth**, under **Date of birth**;
- **Withdraw consent**, which locks the account straight away;
- **Delete their data**: type DELETE, then confirm.

After withdrawing, turning the account back on needs approved documents again.

## Common questions
### Why do you need my ID?
A link in an email only proves someone could open that inbox. A child could type a second email of their own and approve themselves. Your ID plus a document naming you as the parent shows a real parent approved, as India's data protection law asks for children's accounts.

### Can both parents approve?
One approval is enough. The parent who sent the documents is the linked parent. To change that, the linked parent withdraws consent first, then the other parent approves.

### What does a locked account mean?
The child can still sign in, but their profile is hidden, and following and messages are off. If nobody approves within 30 days, the account and its personal data are deleted.

### Does deleting remove my child's match results?
Their name, details, photo and ID proofs are erased. Scores of matches they played stay in their teams' histories as "Deleted player", so standings stay correct.
