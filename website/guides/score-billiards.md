English billiards uses the red and two cue balls. A pot red or an in-off red (the cue ball goes in off the red) scores 3; a cannon (the cue ball hits both other balls), a pot white or an in-off white scores 2. The app follows the WPBSA rules.

## Before you start
- You must be a scorer of the match, or a host of the match or its tournament. See [Scorers and officials](/guides/scorers-and-officials/).
- For a friendly, tap **🤝 Start a friendly** on **Organize** (or in the **▾** menu on Home). Pick **⚪ Billiards**, **Player 1** and **Player 2**. Billiards is singles only.
- Under **⚪ Billiards rules**, pick the **Format**: **Short format (150-up · best of 5)**, **One game 150-up**, **One game 100-up**, **Long-up 1000**, **Timed game** or **Custom**. **⚙ Customize this format** sets the foul points (2), the hazard and cannon limits and level timed games.
- Pick the **Scoring**: **Shot by shot (breaks and stats)** or **Frame (or game) scores only**. Tap **▶ Create & score now**.

In a one-game match the app says "game" instead of "frame".

## Step by step
1. Open the match → **Scoring** tab and tap **▶ Start the match**.
2. Under **Who breaks off? (the toss · then it alternates)**, tap the player who breaks off. Player 1 is picked until you change it.
3. Tap one button per scoring stroke: **Pot red · 3**, **In-off red · 3**, **Cannon · 2** or **Pot white · 2**. The top shows "Asha at the table · break 17" and what each player needs to win.
4. Did one stroke score twice, such as a cannon and a pot red? Tap **Combo…**, tap each part (**Pot red 3**, **Cannon 2** …), then **Record stroke · 5 points**. An in-off white is scored here too.
5. When the visit ends, tap **Missed** or **Safety**. The turn passes.
6. A tap that reaches the target asks first, for example "End frame 1?". Tap **Yes, end frame**. The frame is recorded at the target, such as 150–87, but the break keeps every point.

## Fouls
Tap **Foul…**, optionally the kind (such as **Missed every ball**, **Coup (cue ball potted without a hit)**, **Push stroke** or **Didn't cross the baulk line**), then **Record foul · 2 to Ravi**. The other player gets the points and the turn. Points scored earlier in the break still count.

> **Note:** **Missed** means a scoring attempt failed: no penalty. A stroke that hits no ball at all is a foul: tap **Foul…** → **Missed every ball**.

## Hazard and cannon limits
A hazard is a pot or an in-off. The standard limits are 15 hazards in a row without a cannon, and 75 cannons in a row without a hazard. Five strokes before a limit, a warning shows, for example "⚠️ 10 hazards in a row: 5 more allowed, then a cannon is needed." At the limit the buttons read, for example, **Pot red · foul now**: that stroke is a foul. A stroke with a hazard and a cannon, or a new visit, starts both counts again.

## Timed games
Tap **⏱ Time called** when the referee calls time. The leader wins. If the scores are level, the app asks first, then plays sudden death: the next score or foul wins. If the organiser chose **A draw**, a level game is drawn.

## Quick mode: game scores only
With **Frame (or game) scores only**, each frame shows "Frame 1 final score". Type both scores and tap **Save frame 1**. In a points game the winner must reach the target and the loser must not; a score past the target is recorded at it. In shot-by-shot mode, **Enter this frame's final score only** works before a frame's first stroke.

**Frame options: concede, award** records a concession, or a frame the referee awards for **Conduct**, **Time** or **Other**.

## Fix a mistake
- Tap **↶ Undo**. It names what it takes back, for example "↶ Undo: pot red + cannon · break 5".
- A finished frame is wrong? On the **Scoring** tab, tap **✎ Correct a finished frame** → **✎ Change frame 2**, type the score, then **Use this score** and **Update score (1)**. Later frames stay as played.
- After the match, use **✏️ Correct this match** on the **Info** tab. See [Correct a match after full time](/guides/correct-a-finished-match/).

## The commentary and the stats
- **Commentary** tells each visit, newest first, with its strokes: "Asha — break of 23 (5 red pots, 4 cannons)". 🔥 marks a 50+ break, 💯 a century.
- **Stats** compares the players: highest break, average (points per visit), centuries, each kind of stroke, safeties, missed attempts and fouls. **Breaks** lists breaks, biggest first (chips **25+**, **50+**, **100+**). **Frame by frame** shows each frame's score, how it ended and the best breaks.

## Records and profiles
A finished match saves its records: highest breaks, century breaks (100+) and biggest frame wins. See [See a tournament's records](/guides/tournament-records/).

Profiles add frames won, highest break, average, centuries and double centuries (200+); breaks count only from shot-by-shot games.
