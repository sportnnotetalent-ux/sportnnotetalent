A verified school shows **✓ Verified by SportnNote** on its page and on the certificates it issues, and its admins can confirm students' dates of birth from school records. Verification proves two things: that the school is real and recognised, and that you speak for it. SportnNote support checks each school by hand, usually within a day.

## Before you start
- You need to be an Owner or Admin of the school's community on SportnNote.
- Have the institution's official code ready:

| Institution | Official code |
|---|---|
| School, or junior / PU college | **UDISE+ code** (11 digits) |
| College | **AISHE code** starting C- (for example C-12345) |
| University | **AISHE code** starting U- (for example U-0306) |
| Polytechnic or other institute (nursing, teacher training …) | **AISHE code**, usually starting S- (for example S-797) |
| ITI | **ITI code** from NCVT MIS (for example GU29000014) |

- Have a photo or PDF of one recognition document, such as the board affiliation letter.
- Verification never stops you running events. Without it, everything still works.

## Registered or informal
When you create a community, the app asks **Is it a registered organisation?** Choose **🏛️ Registered organisation** for a school, college, university, institute, company, club or society, or **👥 Informal group** for friends, a neighbourhood or an office team. An informal group needs no verification, and can't be an educational institution.

Pick the type that fits: **School**, **Junior / PU college**, **College**, **University**, **Polytechnic / institute** or **ITI**. Each one keeps class or year groups for its members, so students move up a year and graduate after the class you set (for example **Grade 12** for a junior college, **Final year** for a university).

## Step by step
1. Open your school's page, stay on the **Details** tab, and on the **🛡️ Verification** card tap **🛡️ Get verified**.
2. **Official ID:** type the UDISE+ or AISHE code. A school can also pick its board, such as **CBSE**, and add that code. Tap **Save**.
3. **Recognition document:** pick the kind, such as **Board affiliation letter** or **State recognition (RTE) certificate**, then tap **📎 Upload the document (image / PDF)**.
4. **Show you're authorised by the school:** choose one way (see below).
5. Tap **Send to SportnNote**. The page shows **📨 With SportnNote support** until it's checked, and you get a notification with the answer.

> **Tip:** Use the school's name exactly as it's registered. Support compares it with the public register.

## Three ways to show you're authorised
A document proves the school exists, but anyone can download one from a school website. This step proves you speak for the school.
- **✉️ Official school email:** type an address the school uses officially, tap **Email me a code**, then type the code and tap **Confirm the code**. This is the fastest way. A free mailbox like Gmail is fine if it's the address the school lists publicly.
- **📄 Letter signed by the Head:** tap **📄 Print the letter template**, print it on school letterhead, and get it signed and stamped by the Principal or Head. Then tap **📎 Upload the signed letter**. Support calls the school's published office number to confirm it.
- **👤 The Head confirms:** type the Principal or Head's name and official email, then tap **Email the Head**. They open the link, check your name, and tap **✓ Yes, they’re authorised**. You can send the request before they answer; support waits for them.

## What support checks
Support looks the code up in the public register (Know Your School for UDISE+, AISHE for colleges), checks the document is genuine and has the same name, and checks your proof of authority. If something doesn't match, it's sent back with a reason, such as "Document unclear or not accepted". Tap **Fix it and send again** on the school's page.

## After it's verified
- **✓ Verified by SportnNote** shows on your page.
- You can tap **📋 Confirm members’ dates of birth** to confirm a whole class at once. See [Require checked ages for a tournament](/guides/players-age-rule/).
- The official code is now locked to your school: nobody else can verify another community with it.
- Renaming the school, changing its type or its official code removes the ✓, so it would need verifying again.

## Common questions
### Who sees my documents?
Only SportnNote support. They're deleted 30 days after the decision.

### Someone else already created our school. What now?
If it's verified, the app says so when you type the code. Ask its admins to add you. If it isn't verified, verification decides who represents the school.

### Our school isn't in India.
Pick your school system when you create the community (or change it on the community page). The code it asks for follows the system:
- **UK:** the school URN (England), or the Scotland, Wales or Northern Ireland reference number.
- **US:** the NCES school ID (public schools: 12 digits; private schools: the 8-character PSS ID).
- **Singapore:** the MOE school code or the UEN.
- **Malaysia:** the MOE school code or the SSM number.
- **Australia:** the ACARA school ID (from My School) or the ABN.
- **Gulf:** the education regulator's licence (KHDA, ADEK, SPEA, MOE …); Indian-curriculum schools can give their CBSE affiliation number instead.

If none fits, choose **Other register** and type the number from the register that lists your school.

### What about clubs and other organisations?
Clubs, academies, associations, companies, NGOs, hospitals and housing societies verify in the same three steps with their own registration number. See [Verify your club, academy or organisation](/guides/verify-your-organisation/).
