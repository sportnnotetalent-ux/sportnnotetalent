A selector, coach or scholarship panel wants proof of what you've played and won, not a list you typed yourself. Your career passport puts your verified record on one page: your official matches in every sport, the honours on certificates organisers issued to you, and how your age was checked, ready to share by link or QR code, or print. Nobody can type results onto it: the matches were scored live on SportnNote, and every honour has a certificate code anyone can check.

## Before you start
- Only the player, or their linked parent or guardian, can share, print or reset a passport. Anyone with the link can view it, signed in or not.
- You need a player profile. The passport fills in from your tournament matches and certificates, so a new profile starts empty.
- For the age badge, your age needs checking first. On your profile, the **🛡️ Age & guardian verification** card has **📎 Upload proof of DOB (image / PDF)**. Your school or club, or your parent, may already have confirmed it.

## Step by step
1. Open the **Profile** tab. On the **🪪 Career passport** card, tap **Open my passport**. The first time, this makes your passport's link.
2. Under **Share it** you'll see a QR code and your passport code, for example `ABCD-E234-56`. Someone next to you can scan the QR with their phone camera.
3. Tap **↗ Share passport link** and send it by WhatsApp, Messages, email or any chat app. The link looks like `app.sportnnote.in/pp/ABCDE23456`. On a computer, the app opens WhatsApp or copies the message instead.
4. Tap **🖨 Print / Save as PDF**. On the web, the passport opens in a new tab; press **🖨 Print / Save as PDF** at the top, then pick a printer or save it as a PDF. In the phone app, your phone's print screen opens.
5. The printed page is A4 (one page for most players), with a QR code that opens the live record.

> **Tip:** Send the link rather than a screenshot. The link always shows your latest record, and each honour can be checked.

## For a parent
You can share your child's passport from your own account. If you aren't linked yet, see [Parent or guardian consent](/guides/parent-guardian-consent/).

1. Sign in with your own account and go to **Settings → Link as a parent/guardian**.
2. Next to your child's name, tap **Manage**.
3. Tap **🪪 Their career passport**. You get the same page and the same **Share it** buttons your child has.

On the same **Manage** page, you can confirm your child's date of birth with **✓ Yes, confirm their date of birth**. Their passport then shows "Age confirmed by their parent".

## What it shows
- At the top: the player's name, how their age was checked ("Age verified by document", "Age confirmed by their school / club" or "Age confirmed by their parent") and the year they joined, for example "On SportnNote since 2026".
- Four tiles: **Official matches**, **Titles** (champion certificates), **Podiums & medals** (runner-up, third place and medals) and **Awards**. The overall record sits under them, for example "8W 2D 1L".
- **Official record by sport**: one row per sport, most played first, with the number of matches, the record and highlights such as goals and assists. Event sports such as athletics and swimming show no win-loss record.
- **Honours**: each certificate with its event, team, date and who issued it. Tap **✓ Check certificate** to see whether it's genuine or has been revoked. See [Issue certificates anyone can check](/guides/certificates/) for how organisers issue them.

What it leaves out:
- Friendlies. Official means a tournament match or event scored on SportnNote. Cancelled matches don't count.
- Participation certificates. Honours are titles, podiums, medals and awards only, and a revoked certificate drops off.
- Opponents' names. The record shows your results, not who you played.

## Common questions
### Why does my child's passport show initials?
To anyone else, a player who is under 18, or has no date of birth on their profile, shows as initials, such as "A. K.". The player and their linked parent see the full name, and so does the printed copy, because only they can print it.

### How do I stop an old link working?
Under **Share it**, tap **Make a new link**, then **Yes, new link**. The old link and QR stop working straight away, and anyone who opens them sees "✗ No passport with that code". Send the new link to the people who should still see it.

> **Important:** A new link also stops the QR on any copy you printed before. Print it again afterwards.

### Can people find my passport without the link?
No. It isn't listed or searchable anywhere, so who sees your record and honours is your choice.

### What does a selector see?
The same page, without the **Share it** card. They don't need an account. With only the code, they can go to `app.sportnnote.in/pp/`, type it into **Passport code** and tap **Open**.

### Why is the age badge missing?
Either your age hasn't been checked yet, or your date of birth changed after a school, club or parent confirmed it. A confirmation only counts while the date of birth still matches.
