Every cricket match has the same tabs as every other sport: **Info**, **Commentary**, **Stats** (the **Scorecard** first, then **Match stats**), **Summary** and, for the scorer, **Scoring**. The score sits above all of them. The commentary and stats come from the balls you already score, so they cost the scorer nothing. If you want more depth, such as a pitch map or how often batters were beaten, switch it on under **Stats to track**. Every extra tap is optional.

## Before you start
- Anyone who can see the match can read the Commentary and Stats tabs.
- To track extra detail, you must score the match (a listed scorer or a host). Organisers can switch it on for a whole tournament under **⚙ Customize this format**.

## The Commentary tab
- On top, **This over** shows the balls of the over in progress and, in a chase, what's still needed: runs, balls, the target, the required rate and the live DLS par.
- Each ball reads like a broadcaster's commentary, for example "12.4 Bumrah to Kohli, FOUR, short of a length outside off, pulled to deep mid-wicket". The score after the ball is shown under it.
- Balls are grouped by over, newest first. Each over shows its runs, the score, the run rate, the two batters, the bowler's figures and, in a chase, what's still needed.
- Wickets show how the batter was out and their final line, for example "Kohli c Rahul b Bumrah 45 (31b 4x4 1x6) SR 145.16".
- Fifties, hundreds, 50 partnerships, five-wicket hauls, new batters, the end of the powerplay, rain and rule changes appear in their place.
- Use the chips to see **All**, **Wickets**, **4s & 6s**, or just the **Overs** summaries. Tap **Show earlier overs** for more.
- The time of day appears on balls scored with the latest app.

## The Stats tab
The **Scorecard** opens first (see [Read the scorecard and career stats](/guides/cricket-scorecard-and-stats/)). Tap **Match stats** for:
- **Compare**: both teams side by side, with bars. It shows runs, run rate, 4s, 6s, boundary %, dot-ball %, scoring-shot %, balls per boundary, economy, how the wickets fell, extras and fielding. Pick **Powerplay**, **Middle** or **Death** to compare one phase.
- **Batting** and **Bowling**: each player's strike rate, dot %, boundary %, economy in each phase, 4s and 6s conceded, wides and no-balls.
- **Phases**: runs, run rate, dot % and boundaries for the powerplay, middle and death overs of each innings.
- **Matchups**: pick a batter to see their runs, balls, dots and dismissals against each bowler.
- **Graphs**: the worm (runs by over, with wickets marked) and the Manhattan (runs in each over) for both innings.
- **Pitch map** and **Shots** appear when the scorer tracked them.

> **Note:** Phases follow the format. The powerplay is the format's own; The Hundred uses 25 balls. The death overs are the last quarter of the innings (overs 16–20 in a T20), or the last 10 overs from 40-over games up. Test and timeless matches have no phases.

## Step by step: track extra detail
1. Open the match → **Info** → **⚙️ Match rules** (or **☰ Quick options** → ⚙️ while scoring).
2. Under **Stats to track**, tap what you want: **Wagon wheel**, **Pitch map (line & length)**, **Shot control (beaten / edged)**, **Ball speed (speed gun)** or **Bowler type (pace / spin)**.
3. Tap **Apply** (before the first ball) or **Apply from next ball** (during the match).
4. Score the ball as usual. Under the scoring buttons, a card asks "📍 Ball 12.4 — detail (optional)".
5. Tap the length and line, and **Beaten**, **Edged** or **Mistimed** for a false shot. Type the speed if you have a speed gun.
6. Tap **Done**, or just bowl the next ball. Tap a chip again to clear it.

> **Tip:** With shot control on, a ball you don't tag counts as "in control". Only tag the false shots.

## Bowler type
- With **Bowler type** on, a row appears under "Bowling: …". Tap **Fast**, **Medium**, **Finger spin** or **Wrist spin** (and **Left-arm** if needed), once per bowler.
- If the player's cricket profile already has a **Bowling type**, the app fills it in for free, even with the setting off. Players can add it under **Edit profile**.
- Batters then get runs and strike rate against pace and against spin.

## Where a catch was taken
With the **Wagon wheel** on, the detail card after a catch asks "Where was the catch taken?". Tap the field. The commentary then reads "OUT! Caught at long-on".

## What reaches the profile
These figures go to careers only from matches that tracked them. A player who never had them tracked sees no row, never a 0.
- **Batting**: strike rate and average against pace and spin, and control %.
- **Bowling**: false shots induced %, fastest ball and average speed.
- Rows say how many innings they cover, for example "3 of 10".

## Common questions
### Does tracking slow down scoring?
No. The run buttons don't move, and every detail tap is optional. Skip a ball, or the whole card, and scoring carries on.

### Can I fix a detail later?
Yes, until the next ball: tap the right chip. **↶ Undo** also takes it back ("Undo: ball detail").

### Why do older matches show no times?
Times come from the scoring phone and older matches didn't save them. Their commentary, stats and graphs still work.
