A school adds its students once, then updates them every year. **Add students** takes the list straight from your school system or a spreadsheet: the whole school at once, one class, or one section. It recognises the students you already have, so the same child is never added twice, and next year's list moves everyone up a class.

## Before you start
- You're an Owner or Admin of the school (or college) on SportnNote.
- Have the list as a CSV file, an Excel file (.xlsx), or cells you can copy and paste.

## Step by step
1. Open your school → **Members** tab → **📥 Add students**.
2. Under **1. Which students?** choose:
   - **🏫 Whole school**: each row's class and section come from the list's Class and Section columns. Use this to add new students across every class at once, or for the new year's full list.
   - **📚 One class or section**: pick the class (and the section, if it has one). Every row goes into it, so a teacher's list of names and dates of birth is enough.
3. Under **2. The list**, tap **Choose a CSV or Excel file** (on the web), or paste the rows with the header row. Tap **Template** for a ready-made example.
4. Tap **🔎 Check**. The app works out what happens to every row.
5. Look at **3. What happens**, answer any **❓ Need a look** rows, then tap **Save the list**.

## The columns
Any order. Common headers from school systems are recognised.

| Column | Notes |
|---|---|
| Admission no | Your school's own number (also "Adm No", "Scholar No", "GR No", "Student ID"). The surest way to recognise a student. Private to the school's admins. |
| Name | Or First name and Last name. |
| Date of birth | Day first or month first. The app asks if every date fits both ways. |
| Class, Section | "7" and "B", or "7-B" / "VII B" / "Class 7B" in one column. Written the way your school already writes its classes. |
| Gender, House | Optional. |
| Guardian name, email, phone | The parent's contact. A child never gets their own contact details from a list. |

## How students are recognised
Each row is compared with your school's current and past students, in this order:
1. The same **admission number**, even if the name is spelt differently.
2. The same **name and date of birth**.
3. The same **first name and a parent's email or phone**.

What happens to each row:

| In the preview | What it means |
|---|---|
| ＋ New students | Not at your school yet: a profile is made and they join in that class. |
| ↗ Moving to another class | Already your student: their class (and House, if given) changes from today. Their history keeps the old class. |
| ↩ Back at the school | A past student is back. |
| ✎ House or admission number updated | Same class, new details. |
| ✓ Already here, nothing changes | Nothing to do. |
| ❓ Need a look | For example, a student with the same name but another date of birth. Choose **Same student**, **New student** or **Skip**. |
| ✗ Not added | A date that can't be right, no name, or the same student twice in the list. |

## A child who's already on SportnNote
Some children already have a profile, set up by a parent or by a club coach. If a row has the same name, date of birth **and** parent contact as one of them:
- No second profile is made, and your school doesn't see theirs.
- SportnNote emails the parent at the address it already has (not one typed in the list), asking whether your school may add their child.
- The parent agrees or says no on a simple page. No account is needed, and the link works for 30 days.
- When they agree, the child joins your students in the class and House from your list.

> **Note:** An adult student, for example at a college, gets the request themselves.

## Students who've left
A **🏫 Whole school** list also shows **🚪 Not on this list**: current students with a class who aren't on it.
- Nothing happens to them unless you tick **Mark these as left today**.
- Students marked as left stay in past results, teams and records. If they come back, a later list brings them back with their history.

## Common questions
### We teach two sections with the same class name. Does that matter?
No. "Class 7-A" and "Class 7-B" are separate. A list for one section never touches the other.

### Our file has "VIII" in some rows and "8" in others.
They're the same class. The list uses the way it was first written, or the way your school already writes it.

### My file is an old .xls, .ods or Numbers file.
Save it as CSV or Excel (.xlsx) first, or copy the cells and paste them in.

### Can a teacher do this?
Only the school's Owners and Admins, because the list holds children's details. Make the teacher an Admin of the school if they should.
