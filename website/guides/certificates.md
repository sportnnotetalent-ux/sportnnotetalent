A paper certificate is easy to fake. A SportnNote certificate carries a QR code and a short code. Scan it, and SportnNote shows whether it's genuine: what was won, where, when, and who issued it. Organisers issue and print them after the tournament. Players and parents find theirs on their profile.

## Before you start
- Only the tournament's organisers can issue certificates. If you see **⚙ Manage** on the tournament, you're one of them.
- Certificates go to players, not teams. Every player in a team's squad gets their own, so make sure squads have their players.
- To include award winners, publish the awards first. See [Publish tournament awards](/guides/tournament-awards/).
- You can print from the phone app or from the web at [app.sportnnote.in](https://app.sportnnote.in).

## Step by step: issue and print certificates
1. Open the tournament and tap **⚙ Manage**, or open the **Manage** tab.
2. Under Tournament, tap **Certificates**.
3. Each sport has a card with **🏆 Champions**, **🥈 Runners-up** and **🥉 Third place**, filled in from the final and the third-place match. Tap another team to change one, or **None** to leave it empty. A league has no final, so pick the teams yourself.
4. Under **Also include**, choose **🎖️ Awards** (published award winners), **🥇 Meet medals** (a meet's medallists) or **🎽 Participation (everyone)** (every player on every team). Participation is off until you turn it on.
5. The line under the cards says how many are ready, for example "28 new certificates ready to issue."
6. Tap the button at the bottom, for example "🏅 Issue 28 certificates".
7. Tap **🖨 Print all**, or tap one of the chips below it to print one kind only, such as just the champions.

Each team chip shows its squad size, for example "Falcons FC · 14".

On the web, the certificates open in a new tab, one A4 landscape page each. Tap **🖨 Print / Save as PDF**. In the phone app, your phone's print screen opens, and you can save a PDF there too.

Each certificate shows the player's full name, the achievement, event, team, date, issuer (your school, club or organiser name), a line to sign, and the QR code with its code, such as `ABCD-E234-56`.

> **Tip:** You can come back later. Certificates already issued stay as they are, and the button only issues new ones, such as awards you published afterwards.

> **Note:** If your phone says printing isn't available, open [app.sportnnote.in](https://app.sportnnote.in) on a computer and print from there.

## Find and share your certificate
For players and parents:
1. Go to **Profile**.
2. Find the **📜 Certificates** card. It lists your certificates and, if you're a parent linked to your child, your child's too. It appears once there's at least one.
3. Tap **🖨 Print** to print it or save a PDF.
4. Tap **↗ Share check link** to send it on WhatsApp, Messages, email or any chat app. Anyone with the link can check it's genuine.

## Check a certificate
Anyone can do this, without an account or the app.
1. Scan the QR code with any phone camera and open the link.
2. A real certificate shows "✓ Genuine SportnNote certificate", with who it was awarded to, the achievement, event, team, date, issuer and code.
3. Compare these with the paper. If anything differs, the paper has been changed.

No camera? Type the web address printed next to the QR code into any browser: app.sportnnote.in/c/ followed by the code. If the page says "✗ No certificate with that code", check the code, type it into **Certificate code** and tap **Check**. Codes never use O, I, 0 or 1, so they're hard to misread. Dashes and capitals don't matter.

A withdrawn certificate shows "⚠ This certificate was revoked". "✓ verified school / club" next to the issuer means the host school or club is verified on SportnNote.

## Common questions

### Why does the check page show initials, not the name?
To protect children. A player under 18, or one whose date of birth isn't known, shows as initials, such as "A. K.". The paper has the full name, so check the initials match.

### A name is spelt wrong. Can I fix it?
Yes. The name comes from the player's details each time you print. Correct it (see [Fix a player's name, shirt number or photo](/guides/fix-a-players-details/)), then print again. The code stays the same.

### I gave certificates to the wrong team. What now?
Pick the right team on the sport's card and issue the new certificates. Then tap **Show each certificate (codes, revoke)**, tap **Revoke** next to each wrong one, and confirm with **Revoke**. Scanning it then shows it was revoked, the player's profile shows "Revoked by the organisers", and **🖨 Print all** leaves it out.

> **Important:** Revoking can't be undone in the app, and that player can't be given the same certificate again. Check before you tap **Revoke**.

### Can a player get the same certificate twice?
No. It's one certificate per player per achievement, so issuing again never makes copies.

### Can certificates be faked?
Someone can photocopy the paper, but not the record in SportnNote. A made-up code shows "✗ No certificate with that code", and an edited certificate won't match the check page. Codes have 10 random characters, so guessing one is practically impossible.
