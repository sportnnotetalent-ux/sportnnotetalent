The first time you sign in, SportnNote asks a few quick questions. It takes about a minute, and **Skip** is at the top of every step. If you're at the ground and need to start a match, tap it and carry on. Your answers go straight into your profile, so you never fill them in twice. Home then shows matches near you.

## Step by step: the first questions
1. **How will you use SportnNote?** Pick any of **I play**, **I organise**, **I score**, **I'm a parent** or **I follow sport**.
2. **Your sports.** Tap every sport you play or follow.
3. **One short card per sport** (players only):
   - your **position** (up to three, e.g. Striker and Winger);
   - your **playing side**, such as preferred foot, batting hand and bowling arm, or playing hand. Leave anything you'd rather not say.
4. **Where are you based?** Type your city or town and pick it from the list. Old names work too, e.g. "Bangalore" or "Gurgaon".
5. **See live matches near you.** Tap **Allow location** or **Not now**.
6. **Your Home is ready.** Pick what to do first: run a tournament, score a match, or go to your Home.

> **Tip:** Skipped it? Home shows a small **Finish setting up** card. Tap **Continue** to pick up where you left off, or **Not now** to hide it for a week.

## Matches near you on Home
Home's **Near you** section shows matches in your sports:
- **Live** matches first:
  - with location on, **nearest first**, e.g. "3.2 km away";
  - without it, the **most watched in your city first**.
- If nothing is live: matches **starting soon** (next 24 hours) and **just finished** (last 2 days).
- It looks within 50 km, then 150 km. If nothing in your sports is on, it shows every sport and says so.
- Tap a sport above the cards to see only that sport.

Each match counts its views, one per person a day. Once a match passes 25 views, it shows the count, e.g. "340 views".

## Location: what we use and what we don't
- Only **while you're using the app**, never in the background.
- **Approximate** location, rounded to about 1 km, used only to sort matches. It's **never stored**.
- Players under 18 aren't asked. They see matches in their city.
- Changed your mind? Use your phone's settings for SportnNote, or the **Use my location** link under Near you.

## Change your answers
1. Go to **Settings → Personalise my Home**.
2. Your earlier answers are already filled in. Change anything and tap through.

Positions and playing sides also show on your profile, under each sport. You can edit them there too.

## Common questions
### Do I have to answer?
No. Every question can be skipped, and you can answer later from Settings.

### Who sees my answers?
Your sports, positions and city show on your profile like before. They're hidden from strangers for players under 18. How you use the app and the sports you follow are private.
