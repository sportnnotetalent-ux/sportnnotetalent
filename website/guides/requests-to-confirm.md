Anyone can type a name onto their team list. That alone doesn't share your child's details. When someone else adds your child, or you, or a player you added, to their team, club, community or event, SportnNote asks you first. Until you confirm, that group's people see only initials. The same goes for a friendly that another team's host sets up against a team you run.

## Before you start
- You'll be asked about a player if you are that player, their parent (with your account linked to theirs), or the person who added them and they haven't joined yet.
- You'll be asked about a friendly if you manage the team in it: its captain, vice-captain, an admin, staff, or an Owner or Admin of its club or school.

## Step by step
1. Open **Home**. When something is waiting, the **🤝 Requests to confirm** card is at the top.
2. Read each request: who added which player, and to what. For example, "Coach Shah added Asha Rao to the team Shah U12".
3. Tap **Confirm** if it's right. From then on, the group's people see the player's full name and profile, as for any teammate.
4. Tap **Not us** if it isn't your child, or you don't want them there. The app asks once more, and **No, keep it** is the safe choice. Then the player is taken off that team, club or community, and that group can't ask again.
5. For a friendly, tap **Accept** or **Decline**. Accepting lets the match's host and scorers see your players' full names. Declining keeps them as initials. The match itself isn't changed.

> **Note:** A school's own staff adding their students, a parent's own team, and teams you made yourself never need confirming. Everything that was on SportnNote before 28 October 2026 counts as confirmed.

## Did a coach add you by phone?
If a coach or school added you with your mobile number, and you then signed up with your email, your number couldn't go on your new profile because the coach-made one already has it. Home then shows **📱 Did a coach add you by phone?**
1. Tap **Text me a code**. An SMS with a 6-digit code goes to that number.
2. Enter the code and tap **Confirm**.
3. If the coach-made profile is yours, it becomes your profile, with its matches, teams and stats.

For safety, support links it by hand instead when:
- the name on it isn't yours;
- the birth year differs;
- both profiles already have matches or teams.

This stops a parent's number on a child's profile from giving the parent the child's profile. Contact support from **Settings** if you're told this.

## Common questions
### I tapped Not us by mistake.
Contact support from **Settings**. The coach can't fix it by adding the player again, because that group won't ask a second time.

### Why does my child show as initials on another coach's team?
Nobody on your side has confirmed that team yet. Look for the request on **Home**.

### I'm an adult. Do I need to confirm too?
You get the same requests. Adults' names are public anyway, so confirming mainly tells the group it's really you.

### Can I take my child off a team later?
Yes. Ask the team's coach, or contact support.

### Is this the same as a tournament invite?
No. When an organiser enters your team in their tournament, it shows as an invite on the tournament's **Teams** tab, and your team's manager accepts it there.
