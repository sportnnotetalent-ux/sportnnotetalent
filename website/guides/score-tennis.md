You tap each point; the app handles 15-30-40, deuce, tiebreaks and serve.

> **Tip:** Team match? See [Run a team tie](/guides/run-team-ties/).

## Before you start
- You must be a scorer or host of the match or its tournament. See [Scorers and officials](/guides/scorers-and-officials/).
- **Format**: **Best of 3 sets**, **2 sets + match tiebreak (3rd to 10)**, **One set**, **Short sets (to 4, best of 3)**, **Short sets to 4, no deuce (Fast4)**, **One long set to 8 (pro set)**, **Doubles: no deuce, 3rd set is a tiebreak to 10**, **Match tiebreak only (first to 10)**, **Best of 5 sets**, **Best of 5, tiebreak at 6-6 in the 5th (Grand Slam)** or **Custom**.
- The **Doubles** preset is the standard ITF, ATP and WTA doubles format: no-ad games, two sets with tiebreaks, and a 10-point match tiebreak instead of a third set.
- **At deuce**: **Advantage (classic)** or **No-ad (deciding point)**.
- **⚙ Customize this format**: **Deciding set** (full set or **10-point match tiebreak**) and **Deciding-set tiebreak**, e.g. **10-point at 6-6 (Grand Slam)**.
- Under **Players**, pick **Singles** or **Doubles**.

## Step by step: score a point
1. Open the match → **Scoring** tab.
2. Under **Who serves first?**, tap the serving side. Until you do, the point buttons stay grey.
3. In doubles, under "Serving order — set 1", tap who serves first in each pair (again every set).
4. The serving line names the server, e.g. "Serving: Asha Rao", and that side's button shows a **SERVING** tag.
5. After each point, tap the big button of the side that won it. Each is in the team colour and named for the side (singles: the player's name).
6. In doubles, to credit one player (optional), tap **credit a player** or long-press a side.
7. Ace or double fault: tap the smaller outline buttons below, **🎯 Ace · Asha Rao** or **⚠️ Double fault · Asha Rao → point Blue House**. Both go to the server.

## How the app follows the serve
- Serve changes every game, across sets.
- In doubles, partners alternate their pair's service games, tiebreaks included (ITF rule).
- Wrong first server? Tap **Fix who served first** under the serving line. Only the serve changes; the score stays.

## Change ends and tiebreak cues
The app works out from the score when players change ends.
- After every odd game, counted through the whole match, a "↔ Change ends" banner appears above the point buttons, for example "↔ Change ends · 90 s changeover". After the first game of a set it says "no rest after the first game", and at the end of a set "set break (120 s)".
- After a set with an even number of games, it shows "⏸ Set break (120 s) · ends change after game 1".
- In a tiebreak, ends change every 6 points, and a banner gives the rule, e.g. "Match tiebreak to 10".


## Reading the board
POINTS shows the current game, with a games column per set and a 🎾 dot by the server. A tiebreak set shows the loser's points small (7 and 6⁴). The result reads like "6-4, 3-6, 7-6(4)".

A chip under the board shows **MATCH POINT**, **SET POINT** or **BREAK POINT** with the side's name.

## Match stats
The **Stats** tab's "Match stats" (per match or set) has tabs:
- **Key stats**: Service (aces, double faults, and with the options below 1st serve in, win 1st / 2nd serve, fastest serve and serve averages), Winners & errors, Points won (break points, net points, receiving and total points) and Games (holds, breaks, break points saved, streaks).
- **Serve & return**: aces, service and return points played and won, and return points in play. With 1st / 2nd serve tracked, switch between **Total**, **1st serve** and **2nd serve**.
- **Rally** (with point detail): forehand and backhand winners, unforced and forced errors by shot, and a stroke summary.
- **Rally length** (with rally length tracked): points won after 0–4, 5–8 and 9+ shots.

A row only shows when the match tracked it. "Return points in play" counts serves that landed in (double faults are left out) where the return came back into play. The "Box score" lists each player's points, aces and double faults (**DF**, counted against the server who served them).

## Extra stats (optional)
Scoring stays one tap per point. To track more, open **⚙️ Scoring settings** and pick under **Stats to track**, or tap **Choose** next to "📊 Extra stats" under the point buttons. All are off by default. A change during play counts from the next point.
- **Point detail**: how the point ended.
- **1st / 2nd serve**: points count as 1st serve unless you tap **2nd serve**.
- **Net points**: who came to the net.
- **Rally length**: 0–4, 5–8 or 9+ shots.
- **Serve speed**: the serve's speed in km/h (30–270).

After each point, a "Last point" card appears. Tap what you know, or **Skip**:
1. **Service winner**, **Winner**, **Forced error** or **Unforced error** (Ace and Double fault keep their own buttons).
2. **Forehand** or **Backhand**.
3. The shot, e.g. **Groundstroke**, **Return**, **Volley**, **Passing**, **Drop shot** (optional).
4. With the other options on: **2nd serve**, **At the net** (tap one or both sides), **Rally** length, and type the **Serve speed**.

Winners count for the point winner, errors against the opponent (doubles: pick who under **By:**). The box score adds **W**, **UE**, **FE**.

## Commentary
The **Commentary** tab tells the match game by game, newest first, the game in play on top:
- "Asha Rao wins the game" with the set score, e.g. **1-1**.
- Each point: "Point to Asha Rao", the match time and, if you described it, a line like "Meera Iyer loses the point with a Backhand Forced Error". On the right, the score after the point, server first: **30 - 15**, **40 - AD**, **Game**.
- Code violations and timeouts where they happened, and who served the game.

It updates after every point and after a correction.

## Fix a mistake
- Wrong tap just now? The undo box names the last step, e.g. "Last step: point to Aarav Mehta (30-15)", next to the **↶ Undo** button.
- Older point: tap **Edit** next to **Correct the timeline** on the **Scoring** tab.
1. Tap ✎ on a point to change the winner, type, player or detail. Tap **Save**.
2. Tap ✕ to remove a point; the app asks you to confirm first.
3. To add a missed point, tap ＋ on the point before it, pick the side and tap **Save**.
4. Tap **Done**.

The app replays the match. After the match, see [Correct a match after full time](/guides/correct-a-finished-match/).

## Ending the match
The match ends itself. For an injury, tap **🏁 End match…** → **Retired**. See [End a match early](/guides/end-a-match-early/). Code and time violations, medical timeouts and match time: see [Code violations, cards and timeouts](/guides/racket-conduct-and-timeouts/).

## What goes on the player's profile
The player's tennis page adds **W-L**, **Win %**, **Match play**, **Serve & return** (with fastest serve and serve averages from serve-speed matches) and **Shot making** (from point-detail matches), plus **Rally length** when it was tracked. Tournament leaders include **Fastest serve**. See [Filter a player's career stats](/guides/filter-career-stats/).

## Common questions
### How does a Fast4 set work?
First to 4 games with no-ad games. At 3-3 there is a tiebreak to 5 points; at 4-4 in the tiebreak, the next point wins the set.
