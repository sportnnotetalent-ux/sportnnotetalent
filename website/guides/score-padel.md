Padel uses tennis scoring, with advantage, a golden point or the 2026 Star Point at deuce. You tap who won each point.

## Before you start
- You must be a scorer or host. See [Scorers and officials](/guides/scorers-and-officials/).
- **Format**: **Golden point (best of 3)**, **Star Point (FIP / Premier Padel 2026)**, **Classic (advantage)**, **Short (to 4, match TB)** or **Custom** (**At deuce**, **Games per set**, **Match length**, **Deciding set**).
- Under **Players**, **Doubles** is the default. Add both pairs.

## Step by step: score a point
1. Open the match → **Scoring** tab.
2. Under **Who serves first?**, tap the pair serving first. Until you do, the point buttons stay grey.
3. Under "Serving order — set 1", tap who serves first in each pair (again every set).
4. The serving line names the server, e.g. "Serving: Asha Rao · Red House".
5. After each point, tap the big team-coloured button of the pair that won it.
6. Crediting one player is optional (**credit a player** or long-press a side).
7. Ace or double fault: tap **🎯 Ace · Asha Rao** or **⚠️ Double fault · Asha Rao → point Blue House** under the point buttons. Both go to the current server. An ace counts as an ace even with point detail off; winners and errors stay "not tracked" unless point detail was on.
8. At 40-40 with golden point, the app shows "Golden point — next point wins the game."

## Star Point
FIP and Premier Padel play the Star Point from 2026: advantage is played at most twice; if it is deuce a third time, the next point wins the game.
- At each deuce the app says "Deuce 1 of 3" or "Deuce 2 of 3".
- At the third deuce the app shows "⭐ Star point" and the board shows **⭐ STAR POINT**. The receiving pair chooses who receives.

## Sets, tiebreaks and the match tiebreak
- Sets are won at 6 games by two; at 6-6 a tiebreak to 7 makes it 7-6. Short sets work the same at 4, with the tiebreak at 4-4.
- A match tiebreak replaces the deciding set: "Match tiebreak — first to 10 (win by 2)."

## How the app follows the serve
- Serve changes every game; partners alternate their pair's service games.
- In a tiebreak the first server serves one point, then it changes every two; a pair's second turn goes to the partner (FIP rule).
- Wrong first server? Tap **Fix who served first**. Only the serve changes; the score stays.

## Change ends
A banner above the point buttons says when to change ends:
- After every odd game of the match: "↔ Change ends · 90 s changeover" (no rest after a set's first game; 120 s at a set break).
- After an even set: "⏸ Set break (120 s) · ends change after game 1".
- In a tiebreak or match tiebreak, every 6 points.

The same cues appear in the commentary, after the point that triggered them; a game the receivers won reads "Break of serve".

## Reading the board
POINTS shows the current game, a games column per set (match tiebreak: "TB") and a 🟡 dot by the serving pair.

A chip under the board shows **MATCH POINT**, **SET POINT** or **BREAK POINT** with the pair's name.

## Match stats
The **Stats** tab's "Match stats" (per match or set) has tabs:
- **Key stats**: Serve (aces, double faults, service winners and, with 1st / 2nd serve on, 1st serve in and points won on the 1st / 2nd serve), Winners & errors, Points won (break points, net points, receiving and total points, streaks) and Games (holds, breaks, break points saved, golden / star points won, set and match points saved).
- **Serve**: every service and return figure, plus each player's serve.
- **Shots** (with point detail): winners, unforced or forced errors by shot, for either wing or **Forehand** / **Backhand** only; a stroke summary; errors by type.
- **Rally length** (with rally length tracked): points won after 0–4, 5–8 and 9+ shots, and on serve / on receive.

A tab or row only shows when the match tracked it. Aces from the one-tap **Ace** button count even with point detail off.

## Extra stats (optional)
Scoring stays one tap per point. To track more, open **⚙️ Scoring settings** and pick under **Stats to track**, or tap **Choose** next to "📊 Extra stats" under the point buttons. All are off by default; an organiser can switch them on for every match of a format under **⚙ Customize this format**. A change during play counts from the next point.
- **Point detail**: how the point ended.
- **1st / 2nd serve**: every point counts as a 1st-serve point unless you tap **2nd serve** after it; a double fault counts as a 2nd serve.
- **Net points**: which pair was at the net.
- **Rally length**: 0–4, 5–8 or 9+ shots.

After each point a "Last point" card appears. Tap what you know, or **Skip**:
1. **Ace** or **Service winner** (server won), **Winner**, **Forced error** or **Unforced error**.
2. **Forehand** or **Backhand**.
3. The shot: **Return**, **Volley**, **Bandeja**, **Víbora**, **Smash**, **Por tres** (smash out ×3 / ×4), **Lob**, **Chiquita**, **Bajada**, **Off the glass**, **Drop**.
4. For an unforced error, **Error:** **Net** or **Out**.
5. With the other options on: **2nd serve**, **At the net** (tap one or both pairs) and the **Rally** length.

Errors count against the opponent (pick who under **By:**).

## Commentary
The **Commentary** tab tells the match game by game, newest first, the game in play on top ("Set 1 · Game 4", "Set 2 · Tiebreak", "Set 3 · Match tiebreak"):
- "Red House wins the game" with the set score, e.g. **3-1**, and "Break of serve", "Game and set" or "Game, set and match".
- Each point: "Point to Red House", the match time and, if you described it, a line like "Asha Rao wins the point with a Forehand Bandeja Winner". On the right, the score after the point, server first: **30 - 15**, **40 - 40**, **Game**; "Golden point" or "Star point" on a deciding point.
- Change ends, code violations and who is serving each game.

It updates after every point and after a correction.

## Fix a mistake
- Wrong tap just now? The undo box names the last step, e.g. "Last step: point to Red House (30-15)", next to the **↶ Undo** button.
- Older point: tap **Edit** next to **Correct the timeline** on the **Scoring** tab.
1. Tap ✎ to change a point (winner, type, player, detail), then **Save**.
2. Tap ✕ to remove one (the app asks first).
3. Tap ＋ on the point before a missed one, pick the side, **Save**.
4. Tap **Done**.

The app replays the match. After the match, see [Correct a match after full time](/guides/correct-a-finished-match/).

## Ending the match
The match ends itself. Otherwise tap **🏁 End match…** → **Retired** or **Default**. See [End a match early](/guides/end-a-match-early/). Code violations and match time: see [Code violations, cards and timeouts](/guides/racket-conduct-and-timeouts/).

## What goes on the player's profile
The player's page adds **W-L**, **Win %**, **Match play**, **Serve & return** and **Shot making**; doubles players get **Partners**. See [Filter a player's career stats](/guides/filter-career-stats/).

## Common questions
### Can I score padel singles?
Yes. Pick **Singles** under **Players**.
