Snooker is scored frame by frame, one tap per pot. The app follows the WPBSA rules and keeps the break, points left and snookers needed.

## Before you start
- You must be a scorer of the match, or a host of the match or its tournament. See [Scorers and officials](/guides/scorers-and-officials/).
- For a friendly, tap **🤝 Start a friendly** on **Organize** (or in the **▾** menu on Home). Pick **🔴 Snooker**, **Player 1** and **Player 2**. Snooker is singles only.
- Under **🔴 Snooker rules**, pick the **Format**, such as **15 reds · best of 5**, **10 reds · best of 5** or **6 reds · best of 7**, and the **Scoring**: **Shot by shot (breaks and stats)** or **Frame scores only**. Tap **▶ Create & score now**.

## Step by step
1. Open the match → **Scoring** tab and tap **▶ Start the match**.
2. Under **Who won the toss? (they break off · then it alternates)**, tap the player who won it. Until you tap, nobody is marked and the note says who breaks off as set up. If nobody taps, the Stats tab says "Toss not recorded".
3. Tap each ball as it is potted: **Red 1**, then a colour, such as **Black 7**. Two or three reds in one shot? Tap **2 reds** or **3 reds**.
4. When the visit ends, tap **Missed** (a missed pot) or **Safety**. The turn passes.
5. The top shows "Asha at the table · break 32" and "43 points left on the table · Ravi needs 1 snooker".
6. A tap that ends a frame asks first, for example "End frame 1?". Tap **Yes, end frame**.

The next frame starts with the other player breaking off. To change that for one frame, tap "Ravi breaks off · Change" before its first shot.

## The ball on
The ball on is the ball the player must hit first. While reds are left, a red is on; after a red, any colour, which goes back on its spot. With the reds gone, the colours are on in order, yellow to black. Balls that aren't on are greyed out. If the scores are level after the last black, it is re-spotted: tap who plays first under "Who plays first? (drawn by lots)".

## Fouls
1. Tap **Foul…**.
2. The value starts at the minimum: 4, or the value of the ball on if higher. Tap a higher value (up to 7) if needed.
3. Optionally tap the kind, such as **In-off (cue ball potted)** or **Hit the wrong ball first**.
4. Tap **Foul and a miss** or **Free ball** if the referee called it. If reds went down in the foul, tap how many: they stay off.
5. Tap **Record foul · 4 to Ravi**.

A banner then offers the fouled player's choice: **Play on**, **Make them play again**, **Free ball** and, after a foul and a miss, **Replace the balls**. Tap what they choose.

- Free ball: when a foul leaves the next player snookered, the referee may give one. Any ball can be nominated and scores as the ball on. A button such as **Free ball 1** appears.
- Foul and a miss: the referee rules the player didn't try hard enough to hit the ball on. The app counts them; after the warning, a third shows **Award frame 1 to Ravi**. Tap it only if the referee awards the frame.

## Quick mode: frame scores only
With **Frame scores only**, each frame shows "Frame 1 final score". Type both scores and tap **Save frame 1**. A frame can't end level, and a score above 147 (107 for 10 reds, 75 for 6) asks you to check it. In shot-by-shot mode, **Enter this frame's final score only** works before a frame's first shot.

**Frame options: concede, award, re-rack** records a concession, the referee's award, or a stalemate re-rack (the frame starts again, same player to break off).

## Fix a mistake
- Tap **↶ Undo**. It names what it takes back, for example "↶ Undo: black · break 8".
- A finished frame is wrong? On the **Scoring** tab, tap **✎ Correct a finished frame** → **✎ Change frame 2**, type the score, then **Use this score** and **Update score (1)**. Later frames stay as played.
- After the match, use **✏️ Correct this match** on the **Info** tab. See [Correct a match after full time](/guides/correct-a-finished-match/).

## The commentary and the stats
- **Commentary** tells each visit, newest first: "Asha — break of 54, then missed a pot". 🔥 marks a 50+ break, 💯 a century.
- **Stats** compares the players for the match or one frame: highest break, centuries, points per visit, pot success, safeties and fouls. **Breaks** lists breaks, biggest first (chips **20+**, **50+**, **100+**). **Frame by frame** shows each frame's score, how it ended and the best breaks.

## Records and profiles
A finished match saves its records: highest breaks, century breaks, maximum breaks and biggest frame wins. A maximum is 147 (107 with 10 reds, 75 with 6), without a free ball. See [See a tournament's records](/guides/tournament-records/).

Profiles add frames won, highest break, centuries and pot success; breaks count only from shot-by-shot frames.
