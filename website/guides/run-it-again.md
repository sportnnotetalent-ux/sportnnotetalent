Schools and clubs run the same meet every year. Setting it up from scratch each time means re-typing the sports, the formats, the points, the age groups, the venues and every event. **Run it again** makes next year's edition from this year's in one step. **Copy this setup** does the same for any tournament on SportnNote, including one somebody else organised, so you can run your own event the way theirs was run.

## Before you start
- To **run it again**, you need to be an organiser of the tournament (a host, or an Owner, Admin or Organizer of the community that hosts it).
- Anyone signed in can **copy the setup** of any tournament.
- Neither one copies teams, players, fixtures, entries or results. The new tournament starts empty.

## Run it again (next year's edition)
1. Open the tournament and tap **Manage**.
2. Under **Tournament**, tap **🔁 Run it again**. Once a tournament has finished, its Info tab also shows **🏁 That's a wrap. Run it again?**
3. Check the name and dates. The year in the name moves on (for example "Annual Meet 2026" becomes "Annual Meet 2027", and "Winter League 2025-26" becomes "2026-27"), and the dates move on a year. Change either if this year's edition falls differently.
4. Pick the **Host**. The next edition stays with the community that ran this one, and a tournament you host yourself keeps its co-hosts.
5. Optional: tap **📨 Invite all** to invite last time's teams. Each captain gets a notification and accepts, and each team goes into the same age group as last time.
6. Tap **🔁 Create the 2027 edition**.

The new edition opens on its **Manage** tab, ready for teams. Registration starts closed: open it on the tournament page when you're ready.

> **Tip:** Both editions' pages show **📅 Every edition** (for example "2025 · 2026 · 2027"), so players and parents can look back at earlier years.

## A meet's events
An athletics, swimming, shooting or other meet's events can't be created without their start lists, so they come across as the meet's programme:
- The sport's page shows **🔁 Events from last time**, each with **Set up ›**.
- The entries desk shows them as columns, so you can tick athletes in before the events are set up.
- **Set up ›** opens the event as it was last time, with the line **🔁 Filled in from last time** at the top. You just pick the athletes, and can still change anything.
- Tap **✕** on an event you're not running this time.

### What Set up fills in
Every sport keeps the event, age group and gender, and the timetable slot and venue area. On top of that:
- **Athletics and swimming:** the rounds, bar heights, implements, take-off board, wind gauge and pool length.
- **Road, race walk and cross-country:** the distance, timing, certified course and team scoring (how many score, team size).
- **Combined events:** the events in order, day 2 and the throws' implements.
- **Shooting:** the match length, scoring by series or shot by shot, and the final.
- **Archery:** the bow, round and the match-play bracket.
- **Rowing and canoe:** the boat, distance, lightweight class, lanes, splits and the rounds.
- **Cycling:** the race, distance, laps and sprints, start gap and stages.
- **Weightlifting:** the bodyweight category.

## Copy this setup (any tournament)
1. Open the tournament and scroll to the bottom of its **Info** tab.
2. Tap **📋 Copy this setup to run your own tournament ›**. On your own tournament it's also under **Manage › 📋 Copy this setup**.
3. Type your tournament's name. Someone else's tournament's name isn't filled in, so you give yours its own.
4. Check the dates and pick the **Host**, then tap **📋 Create my tournament**.

## Common questions
### Can I change things after it's created?
Yes. Everything is yours to edit: **Manage › Edit details** for the name, dates, sports and registration, and each sport's settings as usual.

### We ran last year's meet without "Run it again". Can I link the two?
Not yet. Editions are linked when the next one is created with **Run it again**.

### Does the original organiser see that I copied their tournament?
No. A copy is a separate tournament of your own, and nothing about it shows on the original.
