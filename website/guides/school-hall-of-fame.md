Trophies sit in a cabinet, and old results get lost when a teacher moves on. The hall of fame gives your school or club one permanent page of its honours, year by year. Verified honours fill in by themselves from certificates your students earn on SportnNote. You add older honours, and ones from outside events, yourself. It also lists the events your school has hosted.

## Before you start
- To add honours and publish, you need to be an Owner or Admin of the school or club on SportnNote.
- The page is private until it's published. Until then, only your school's members can see it.
- To link a student to an honour, they need to be a member of your school on SportnNote.

## Step by step
1. Open the **Organize** tab and, under **Communities**, tap your school.
2. On the **Details** tab, find the **🏆 Hall of fame** card and tap **Open hall of fame**.
3. To add a past honour, tap **＋ Add an honour**.
4. Type the **Year**, for example `2019`, and tap the kind: Champions, Runners-up, Third place, Medal, Award or Other.
5. Type a **Title**, for example `District Football Champions`. If you like, pick the sport and the level, from School and Zonal up to National and International.
6. Add a **Team (optional)**, such as `U14 Boys`. To name students, search your members and tap each one. Tap a picked name again to remove it.
7. Add a **Note (optional)**, such as the score in the final, and tap **Add to the hall of fame**.
8. When the page is ready, tap **🌐 Publish** and then **Yes, publish**.
9. Tap **↗ Share the link** to send it by WhatsApp, Messages, email or any chat app, or put it on your school's website.

> **Tip:** For a medal, start the title with Gold, Silver or Bronze, for example `Gold, 100 m U16 Girls, State Meet`. The honour then shows the matching medal.

> **Important:** Pick students from your members instead of typing their names. A full name typed into the title, team or note is saved as initials if that student may be under 18.

## Print an honours board
1. Tap **🖨 Print honours board**.
2. On the web, the board opens in a new tab. Tap **🖨 Print / Save as PDF** there. In the phone app, your phone's print screen opens, and you can save a PDF from it.

The A4 board shows your totals, every honour year by year, and a QR code that opens the live page. If you've picked one sport on the page, the board shows only that sport.

> **Note:** Publish before you put the board on a wall. If the page is still private, anyone who scans the QR and isn't a member sees only your school's name and "Not published yet". The board prints names as they appear on your screen, so check it first.

## How verified honours appear
Verified honours come from the certificates organisers issue after a tournament (see [Issue certificates anyone can check](/guides/certificates/)). They appear by themselves, tagged **✓ Verified certificate**.
- Titles, runners-up, third places, medals and awards count. Participation certificates don't.
- Only certificates a student earned while they were a member of your school count. A student who has left keeps the honours they won with you.
- A team title is one honour listing your students in that team, not one line per player. A relay medal works the same way.
- If your school hosted the event, the honour says School event.
- Tap **See event ›** to open the tournament.
- If the organiser revokes a certificate, its honour comes off the page.

Honours you add yourself are tagged **Added by the school**, so readers can tell the two apart.

## Common questions
### Who can see the page?
Before you publish, only your school's members can see it. Anyone else sees your school's name and "Not published yet". Once published, anyone with the link can see it, signed in or not. To hide it again, tap **Make private**.

### Are children's full names shown?
Not to the public. A student who may be under 18 shows as initials, like A. K. Anyone without a date of birth showing they are 18 or over is treated this way.

### How do I change or remove an honour?
For an honour your school added, tap **✎ Edit** under it, make your changes and tap **Save changes**. To delete it, tap **Remove this honour** and confirm. Verified honours can't be edited, because they come from the organiser's certificates.

### What does "School event" mean?
Your school hosted the tournament where the honour was won. Every event your school has hosted is also listed under Events hosted at the bottom of the page.

### Can I show one sport only?
Yes. When the page has honours in more than one sport, sport buttons appear above the years. Tap one to see only that sport, or **All sports** to see everything. The counts at the top follow your choice, apart from events hosted.
