A verified organisation shows **✓ Verified by SportnNote** on its page and on the certificates it issues. Verification proves the organisation is real and registered, and that you speak for it. SportnNote support checks each one by hand, usually within a day. Schools and colleges have their own guide: [Verify your school on SportnNote](/guides/verify-your-school/).

## Before you start
- You need to be an Owner or Admin of the organisation's community on SportnNote.
- It must be set up as a **🏛️ Registered organisation**, with a standard type: Sports club, Sports academy, Sports association, Company, Hospital chain, NGO or Housing society.
- Have one registration number ready (see the table below).
- Have a photo or PDF of one registration document, such as the registration certificate.
- Verification never stops you running events. Without it, everything still works.

## Which number to use

| Type | Any one of these | Who signs or confirms for it |
|---|---|---|
| Sports club | Society or trust registration, CIN, LLPIN, GSTIN, Udyam | President / Secretary |
| Sports academy | Society or trust registration, CIN, LLPIN, GSTIN, Udyam | Director / Owner |
| Sports association | Society or trust registration, CIN | President / Secretary |
| Company | CIN or LLPIN (GSTIN can be added too) | Director / authorised signatory |
| Hospital chain | CIN, LLPIN, trust or society registration | Director / authorised signatory |
| NGO | NGO Darpan ID, society or trust registration, CIN (Section 8) | President / Secretary |
| Housing society | Co-operative society or society registration | Chairman / Secretary |

> **Tip:** Type the number exactly as it's printed. Capitals and spaces are fixed for you. Use the organisation's name exactly as registered: support compares it with the certificate or the public register.

## Step by step
1. Open the organisation's page, stay on the **Details** tab, and on the **🛡️ Verification** card tap **🛡️ Get verified**.
2. **Official ID:** tap the kind of registration you have, for example **Society** or **GST**, type the number, and tap **Save**.
3. **Registration document:** pick the kind, such as **Society / trust registration certificate**, then tap **📎 Upload the document (image / PDF)**.
4. **Show you're authorised:** choose one way (see below).
5. Tap **Send to SportnNote**. The page shows **📨 With SportnNote support** until it's checked, and you get a notification with the answer.

## Three ways to show you're authorised
- **✉️ Official email:** type an address the organisation uses officially, tap **Email me a code**, then type the code and tap **Confirm the code**. This is the fastest way. A free mailbox like Gmail is fine if it's the one the organisation lists publicly.
- **📄 Letter signed by the head:** tap **📄 Print the letter template**, print it on the organisation's letterhead, get it signed (and stamped, if it has a seal) by the President, Secretary, Director or authorised signatory, then tap **📎 Upload the signed letter**. Support calls the organisation's published number to confirm it.
- **👤 The head confirms:** type their name and official email, then tap **Email them**. They open the link and tap **✓ Yes, they're authorised**. You can send the request before they answer; support waits for them.

## What support checks
Support checks three things:
- **The number:** support looks it up in the public register where there is one (MCA for CIN and LLPIN, the GST portal, Udyam, NGO Darpan). Society, trust and co-operative registrations have no national public search, so support compares the number, name and place with your certificate instead.
- **The document:** that it's genuine and shows the same name.
- **Your proof of authority.**

If something doesn't match, it's sent back with a reason, such as "Name doesn't match the register". Tap **Fix it and send again** on the organisation's page.

## After it's verified
- **✓ Verified by SportnNote** shows on your page and on certificates.
- **Clubs, academies and associations** can tap **📋 Confirm members' dates of birth** to confirm members' ages from their own records. See [Require checked ages for a tournament](/guides/players-age-rule/). Companies, hospitals, NGOs and housing societies get the ✓, but don't confirm ages.
- **The national number is locked to your organisation** (CIN, LLPIN, GSTIN, Udyam, NGO Darpan), so nobody else can verify another community with it.
- **Changing details means verifying again:** renaming the organisation, or changing its type or official ID, removes the ✓.

## Common questions
### Who sees my documents?
Only SportnNote support. They're deleted 30 days after the decision.

### Our club isn't registered anywhere. What now?
Choose **👥 Informal group** for the community. Informal groups need no verification, and everything still works. You can switch to **Registered** later on the **🛡️ Verification** card.

### We picked "Custom…" as the type.
Verification needs one of the standard types. Change the type on the community page (Details → edit), then come back to **🛡️ Get verified**.
