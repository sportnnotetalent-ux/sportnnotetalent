Your date of birth decides which age groups you can play in, so some tournaments only accept players whose age has been checked. A parent, your school or club, or a document can check it. A checklist on your profile shows what's done and what to do next.

## Before you start
- Sign in to your own account in [the app](https://app.sportnnote.in).
- Add your date of birth in **✎ Edit profile**. Nothing can be checked without it.
- If you're under 18, add your parent or guardian's name, mobile and email in **✎ Edit profile** too.

## Step by step: follow the checklist
1. Go to **Profile**. The **✅ Ready to play** card shows how many items are done, for example "3 of 6 done".
2. Read the line that starts with "Next:". It's the one thing to do now. Items waiting on someone else, such as a reviewer or your parent, show ⏳ and are skipped.
3. Tap the button under it. **✎ Edit profile** opens your details, and **✉️ Send my parent the link** emails your parent. When it points to a card below, use **Verify** on the contact card or the upload button on the verification card.
4. When every item is ticked, the card tells you you're all set.

What's on the list:
- Under 18: date of birth, parent or guardian added, parent's consent, parent's mobile verified, parent's email verified, and age verified.
- 18 and over: date of birth, your own mobile and email verified, and age verified.

## Three ways to get your age verified
### A parent confirms it
This is the fastest way for under-18s.
- From the email: the consent page asks, for example, "Is Aisha's date of birth 5 Mar 2012?". The parent taps **✓ Yes, that’s right**, then **I agree**.
- From their own account, any time: a linked parent goes to **Settings → Link as a parent/guardian**, taps **Manage** next to the child, and taps **✓ Yes, confirm their date of birth**.

To link your parent's account, tap **📨 Email them a link code** on your profile. Your parent signs in with their own account, opens **Link as a parent/guardian**, types the code and taps **Link**. See [Parent or guardian consent](/guides/parent-guardian-consent/).

### Your school or club confirms it
A school or club that SportnNote has verified can confirm its members' dates of birth from its own records, a whole class at once. Ask your PE teacher, coach or club admin. Your checklist ticks itself once they confirm. This works at any age.

### Upload a document
1. On your profile, find the **🛡️ Age & guardian verification** card.
2. Tap **📎 Upload proof of DOB (image / PDF)** and pick a birth certificate, school ID or passport. A photo or PDF up to 10 MB works.
3. The card shows **⏳ Pending review**. A SportnNote reviewer usually checks it within a few hours, and you get a notification.

The file is deleted 30 days after the decision. Only the result is kept.

## What "age verified by…" means
The last item on your checklist then says how your age was checked:
- "Age verified by document": a reviewer matched your document with the name and date of birth on your profile.
- "Age verified by your school / club": a verified school or club confirmed it from its records.
- "Age verified by your parent": your parent or guardian confirmed it.

Organisers choose what each tournament accepts. A tournament set to "Confirmed" accepts any of the three. One set to "Verified" accepts only a school or club or a document, so a parent's confirmation isn't enough there. Organisers can read [Require checked ages for a tournament](/guides/players-age-rule/).

> **Important:** A confirmation counts only for the date of birth that was confirmed. If you change your date of birth, every confirmation and document approval stops counting, and it has to be checked again.

## If your document is sent back
The card shows **✗ Rejected** with a "Support note" giving the reason, and you get a notification. The reviewer picks one of these reasons or types their own:
- The photo is blurry or cut off
- The date of birth doesn’t match the profile
- The name doesn’t match the profile
- Not an accepted type of document
- The file didn’t upload — please upload it again

Fix the problem, then tap **📎 Re-submit proof (image / PDF)**. If the date of birth or name on your profile is the wrong one, correct it in **✎ Edit profile** first.

## Common questions
### Who can see my document?
Only you and SportnNote's reviewers. Other people see only a ☑️ next to your name.

### "Your account doesn't need a parent or guardian to confirm it." What now?
You see this when consent was already given, for example at sign-up, so no new email is sent. Your parent can still confirm your date of birth from their own account, as described under "A parent confirms it".

### My parent tapped "No, it's wrong". What now?
Fix your date of birth in **✎ Edit profile**. Your parent can then confirm it from **Manage** in their own account.
