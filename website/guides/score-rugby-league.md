Score a rugby league match on one phone: you run the clock and tap what happens; the app adds the points, times sin bins and builds the stats. Rugby league is its own sport in the app, separate from rugby union.

## Before you start
- You must be a scorer or host of the match or its tournament. See [Scorers and officials](/guides/scorers-and-officials/) and [Only one phone scores at a time](/guides/one-scorer-at-a-time/).
- The organiser picks the **Format** (13s, U18, U16, U14, Nines or **Custom**). It sets the half length, the sin bin (10 minutes; 3 in Nines) and the interchanges allowed (8 in senior 13s; unlimited for juniors and Nines).
- Put each team's starting players on the **Info** tab so everyone gets minutes.

## Step by step: run the clock
1. Open the match → **Scoring** and tap **▶ Kick-off**. Tap **⏸ Stop** and **▶ Resume** whenever the referee stops time.
2. At the siren the clock turns red and keeps going (`40:00 +0:20`): play goes on until the ball is dead, so a try on the siren and its conversion still count.
3. At half-time tap **⏭ End H1**, then **▶ Start H2**. **🏁 Full time** sits at the bottom of the controls.
4. Clock wrong? Tap **⏱ Set clock**, type the match time (`52:30`) and tap **Set**.

## Step by step: log the scores
1. **🏉 Try** (4 points): tap the scorer (or **Team try (no scorer)**), then the player who gave the pass, or **No assist**.
2. The conversion follows: **✓ Good** (2 points) or **✗ Missed**, then the kicker; **Not taken** if there's no kick.
3. **🎯 Kick at goal** is a penalty goal (2). **🦶 Field goal** is a drop goal (1 point). Tap **✓ Good** or **✗ Missed**, then the kicker.
4. **🏉 Penalty try**: 4 points to the team. Unlike rugby union, the conversion is still taken, from in front of the posts, so it comes up next.

> **Tip:** Name the kicker every time, misses too: that's what makes goal-kicking % right.

## Sin bins, send-offs and interchanges
- Tap **⏱ Sin bin · off**, then **⏱ Sin bin**, **🟥 Send off** or **📝 Put on report**, then the player. A sin bin is timed on playing time, across half-time too, and the banner shows "Playing 1 short". A send-off is for the rest of the match, not replaced. On report changes nothing on the field.
- Tap **🔁 Interchange**, then **🔁 Interchange**, **🩺 HIA (free)** or **🩸 Blood bin (free)**, who comes off and who comes on. The panel counts interchanges used ("3 of 8"); **↩ Back on** returns an HIA or blood bin player. An interchanged player may come back on later.

## Golden point
- The organiser chooses **If level at full time**: **Draw stands**, **Golden point 2 × 5 min, then the draw stands** (the NRL and Super League regular season) or **Golden point until someone scores** (finals). Tournament knockouts always get the finals rule.
- The first score of any kind wins at once; there's no conversion after a golden-point try. Nines events can switch on **Golden try**: only a try ends extra time.

## Six-tackle sets
Switch on **Six-tackle sets** under **Stats to track** (the match format or **⚙️ Match settings**). A panel appears above the team buttons:
1. At each kick-off tap **Ball:** and the team that has it.
2. Tap the big **Tackle** button after every tackle. The panel reads "Tackle 3 · Lions in possession".
3. When the set ends, tap **🦶 Kick**, **✋ Error**, **⚖ Penalty against** or **🔁 Six again**. The 6th tackle hands the ball over by itself, and a score ends the set. For an intercept, tap **↔ Ball to** the other team.

You get sets, completion rate, tackles per set and possession. A set is complete unless it ends in an error, a penalty against the team with the ball, or a handover on the last tackle.

## More stats to track
All off by default: **Tackles & missed tackles** and **Runs, line breaks & offloads** add a **📋 Detail** button per team; **Errors & penalties conceded** asks the player or **Team (no name)** (with six-tackle sets on, the set's **✋ Error** and **⚖ Penalty against** ask who). Organisers can also switch on **Two-point field goal from 40 m (NRL)** and **Bonus try zone (Nines)**. Stats you don't track never show as 0.

## Commentary, stats and fixes
- The **Commentary** tab is the play-by-play by half and golden point, newest first, with the running score; tap **Key moments** to keep only the big ones. The **Stats** tab has **Match stats** (team v team, by half and category) and the **Box score**.
- **↶ Undo** says what it will remove, for example "Undo Try · Asha Rao (10-6)". **✎ Correct the timeline** lists every moment: **✕** removes one, **✎ Edit** re-enters it at its original time.

## What you get
- Careers and leaderboards: points, tries, try assists, goal-kicking %, field goals, and Top try scorer and Top points scorer awards.
- Tables give 2 points a win and 1 a draw; ties go to points difference, then points scored. A walkover counts 24-0; change it if your competition differs.

## Common questions
### Does a second sin bin send a player off?
Not by itself. If the referee sends the player off, record a send-off.
